# Exercise 9.8 — Water filling with unequal power prices

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 292–293.

## Solution

Assume positive costs and noise variances. Rescale the allocated powers to $`Q_i=\beta_i P_i`$ and the noise levels to $`L_i=\beta_i N_i`$. The constraint is $`Q_1+Q_2\le\beta`$ and the capacity objective is $`\frac12\sum_i\log_2(1+Q_i/L_i)`$.

### (a)
Order the effective levels as $`L_{\min}\le L_{\max}`$. Only the cheaper effective channel is active until the total budget reaches
```math
\beta_{\rm threshold}=L_{\max}-L_{\min}
=|\beta_1N_1-\beta_2N_2|.
```
Above this budget both are used. The complete solution is $`Q_i=(\nu-L_i)_+`$, with $`\sum_iQ_i=\beta`$, followed by $`P_i=Q_i/\beta_i`$.

### (b)
Here $`L_1=3,L_2=4`$, so the threshold is 1. With budget 10, $`2\nu-7=10`$, hence $`\nu=17/2`$. Therefore
```math
P_1=11/2,\qquad P_2=9/4,
```
and
```math
C=\frac12\log_2\left(\frac{17/2}{3}\frac{17/2}{4}\right)
=\frac12\log_2(289/48)
```
approximately $`1.2949815909`$ bits per pair of channel uses. These allocations satisfy $`P_1+2P_2=10`$.

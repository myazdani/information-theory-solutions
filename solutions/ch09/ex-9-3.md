# Exercise 9.3 — A power budget imposed at the output

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 291.

## Solution

Independence and zero noise mean give
```math
\mathbb EY^2=\mathbb EX^2+\sigma^2.
```
If $`P<\sigma^2`$, no input distribution satisfies the constraint, so the channel optimization has an empty feasible set. If $`P=\sigma^2`$, only $`X=0`$ almost surely is feasible and capacity is zero. For $`P>\sigma^2`$, the effective input-power limit is $`P-\sigma^2`$. The Gaussian entropy bound gives
```math
C=\frac12\log_2\left(1+\frac{P-\sigma^2}{\sigma^2}\right)
=\frac12\log_2(P/\sigma^2),
```
attained by an independent zero-mean Gaussian input of variance $`P-\sigma^2`$. Here $`\sigma^2>0`$ is assumed.

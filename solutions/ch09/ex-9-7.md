# Exercise 9.7 — Adding two noisy propagation paths

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 292.

## Solution

### (a)
The antenna sums the two path outputs, so $`Y=2X+Z_1+Z_2`$. The summed noise is Gaussian with variance $`2\sigma^2(1+\rho)`$. For $`\rho>-1`$, dividing the output by 2 gives an equivalent unit-gain channel with noise variance $`\sigma^2(1+\rho)/2`$. Hence
```math
C=\frac12\log_2\left(1+\frac{2P}{\sigma^2(1+\rho)}\right).
```
A zero-mean Gaussian input of variance $`P`$ attains the bound.

### (b)
At $`\rho=0`$, $`C=\frac12\log_2(1+2P/\sigma^2)`$. At $`\rho=1`$, $`C=\frac12\log_2(1+P/\sigma^2)`$. At $`\rho=-1`$, the summed noise vanishes, giving infinite capacity for $`P>0`$ and zero for $`P=0`$. Assume $`\sigma^2>0`$.

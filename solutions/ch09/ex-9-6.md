# Exercise 9.6 — When the second Gaussian channel becomes active

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 291–292.

## Solution

Write $`Q_1+Q_2=2P`$ for the allocated powers. The objective is
```math
\frac12\log_2(1+Q_1/\sigma_1^2)+\frac12\log_2(1+Q_2/\sigma_2^2).
```
Its marginal gains are proportional to $`1/(\sigma_i^2+Q_i)`$. Initially all power goes to the quieter second channel. The first becomes worth using when $`\sigma_2^2+2P=\sigma_1^2`$. Thus the threshold is
```math
P_{\rm threshold}=\frac{\sigma_1^2-\sigma_2^2}{2}.
```
At equality the first allocation is still zero; it is strictly positive above the threshold. In full,
```math
Q_1=\left(P-\frac{\sigma_1^2-\sigma_2^2}{2}\right)_+,
\qquad Q_2=2P-Q_1.
```
The factor of two comes from the problem's total-power convention.

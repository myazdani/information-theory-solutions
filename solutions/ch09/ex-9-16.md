# Exercise 9.16 — Correlation multiplication in Gaussian chains

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 295.

## Solution

Conditional independence gives $`\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z\mid Y)=0`$. Gaussian regression makes the conditional means affine in $`Y`$, with slopes $`\rho_1\sigma_X/\sigma_Y`$ and $`\rho_2\sigma_Z/\sigma_Y`$. The law of total covariance therefore yields $`\operatorname{Corr}(X,Z)=\rho_1\rho_2`$. Thus
```math
I(X;Z)=-\frac12\log_2(1-\rho_1^2\rho_2^2).
```
The finite formula presumes nonzero variances and a product of correlation magnitudes below one. A perfect nondegenerate Gaussian dependence has infinite mutual information, whereas a deterministic endpoint has zero mutual information and an undefined correlation coefficient. This duplicates the computation of Exercise 9.11.

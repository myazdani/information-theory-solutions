# Exercise 9.11 — Mutual information across a Gaussian Markov chain

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 294.

## Solution

For nonzero variances, Gaussian regression and conditional independence imply
```math
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)
=\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbb E[X\mid Y],\mathbb E[Z\mid Y])
=\rho_1\rho_2\sigma_X\sigma_Z.
```
Therefore the correlation between $`X`$ and $`Z`$ is $`\rho_1\rho_2`$, and the bivariate Gaussian entropy formula gives
```math
I(X;Z)=-\frac12\log_2(1-\rho_1^2\rho_2^2).
```
It is infinite if the correlation product has magnitude one with nondeterministic endpoints. If either endpoint is deterministic, the mutual information is zero and its correlation coefficient is not defined. The derivation is the same as in Exercise 8.9.

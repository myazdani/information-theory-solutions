# Exercise 4.35: Concavity of conditional uncertainty along a chain

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 100. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Write $`a_n=H(X_n\mid X_0)`$ and $`a_0=0`$. By stationarity and the Markov property,
```math
a_n-a_{n-1}=H(X_n\mid X_0)-H(X_n\mid X_1)
=I(X_1;X_n\mid X_0).
```
For $`n\ge2`$, expand the same conditional information in two orders:
```math
I(X_1;X_{n-1},X_n\mid X_0)
=I(X_1;X_{n-1}\mid X_0)
=I(X_1;X_n\mid X_0)+I(X_1;X_{n-1}\mid X_0,X_n).
```
The first equality uses conditional independence of $`X_n`$ from the earlier states given $`X_{n-1}`$. Subtracting the increment expressions yields
```math
a_n-2a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}=-I(X_1;X_{n-1}\mid X_0,X_n)\le0.
```
This is precisely the stated discrete concavity. For $`n=2`$, the final information reduces to $`H(X_1\mid X_0,X_2)`$, and the identity remains valid.

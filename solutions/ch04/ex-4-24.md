# Exercise 4.24: Grouping and subsampling a stationary process

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 97. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Suppose $`H(X_1)<\infty`$. Overlapping pairs $`Y_1^n`$ contain exactly the information in $`X_1^{n+1}`$, so $`h(Y)=h(X)`$. Disjoint pairs $`Z_1^n`$ contain exactly $`X_2^{2n+1}`$, so $`h(Z)=2h(X)`$.

For the even subsequence, conditioning on only even past observations cannot give smaller conditional entropy than conditioning on the full past. By stationarity and the conditional-entropy limit,
```math
h(V)=\lim_nH(X_{2n}\mid X_2,X_4,\ldots,X_{2n-2})\ge h(X).
```
Also $`H(X_2,X_4,\ldots,X_{2n})\le H(X_1^{2n})`$, hence $`h(V)\le2h(X)`$.

## (a)
$`h(X)=h(Y)`$.

## (b)
$`h(X)\le h(Z)=2h(X)`$.

## (c)
$`h(X)\le h(V)`$.

## (d)
As printed, the comparison repeats (b) in reverse order: $`h(Z)\ge h(X)`$. The additional useful relation is $`h(V)\le h(Z)`$. The inequalities involving doubled rate are strict when $`h(X)>0`$.

# Exercise 4.13: Adjacent blocks have sublinear shared information

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 93. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Let $`a_n=H(X_1,\ldots,X_n)`$ and suppose the stationary process has finite one-symbol entropy. Stationarity gives both length-$`n`$ blocks entropy $`a_n`$, and their union has entropy $`a_{2n}`$. Hence
```math
\frac1{2n}I(X_1^n;X_{n+1}^{2n})
=\frac{a_n}{n}-\frac{a_{2n}}{2n}\longrightarrow h-h=0,
```
where $`h`$ is the entropy rate. This is an $`o(n)`$ statement: it does not claim that the unnormalized shared information is bounded or tends to zero. Finite block entropies are needed to form the displayed differences.

# Exercise 4.9: The initial state becomes harder to infer

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 92. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

The Markov property gives $`X_0\to X_{n-1}\to X_n`$. Therefore
```math
H(X_0\mid X_n)\ge H(X_0\mid X_{n-1},X_n)=H(X_0\mid X_{n-1}).
```
Only conditioning monotonicity and conditional independence are used. Stationarity is unnecessary.

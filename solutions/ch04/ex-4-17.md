# Exercise 4.17: Mean recurrence time and entropy

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 96. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Assume all $`m`$ alphabet symbols have positive probability; otherwise replace $`m`$ by the size of the positive-probability support.

## (a)
Given $`X_0=x`$, the waiting time $`N`$ is geometric on positive integers with success parameter $`p(x)`$. Hence
```math
\mathbb EN=\sum_xp(x)\mathbb E[N\mid X_0=x]
=\sum_xp(x)\frac1{p(x)}=m.
```

## (b)
Conditional Jensen for the concave logarithm gives
```math
\mathbb E[\log_2N\mid X_0=x]\le\log_2\mathbb E[N\mid X_0=x]=\log_2\frac1{p(x)}.
```
Average over $`x`$ to obtain $`\mathbb E\log_2N\le H(X)`$.

## (c)
Here is the return-time argument for a stationary ergodic process. Work on its two-sided stationary path space with measure-preserving shift $`T`$. For $`A_x=\{X_0=x\}`$, let $`R_x`$ be the first positive return time. The return-time tower levels
```math
T^j(A_x\cap\{R_x>j\}),\qquad j=0,1,2,\ldots,
```
are disjoint up to null sets: each point is labeled by its most recent visit to $`A_x`$ and its elapsed time. Recurrence and ergodicity imply that their union covers almost all path space when $`p(x)>0`$. Their probabilities therefore sum to 1. By shift invariance and the tail-sum formula,
```math
1=\sum_{j\ge0}\Pr(A_x,R_x>j)=p(x)\mathbb E[R_x\mid A_x].
```
Thus $`\mathbb E[N\mid X_0=x]=1/p(x)`$ also in this setting, and averaging again gives $`m`$. A one-sided stationary process admits the same argument in its stationary two-sided extension.

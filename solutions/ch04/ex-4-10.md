# Exercise 4.10: Pairwise independence versus joint entropy

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 92–93. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
The first $`n-1`$ coordinates are independent by construction. For any $`i<n`$, condition on $`X_i`$. Since $`n\ge3`$, at least one other independent fair input remains in the parity defining $`X_n`$, making that parity fair regardless of $`X_i`$. Thus $`X_n`$ is independent of each individual earlier bit as well.

## (b)
Every distinct pair is two independent fair bits, so its joint entropy is 2 bits.

## (c)
The last coordinate is determined by the first $`n-1`$, so
```math
H(X_1,\ldots,X_n)=H(X_1,\ldots,X_{n-1})=n-1.
```
This differs from $`nH(X_1)=n`$, demonstrating that pairwise independence is weaker than mutual independence.

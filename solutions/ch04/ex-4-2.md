# Exercise 4.2: Conditional uncertainty in both time directions

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 88–89. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Let $`a_j`$ denote the entropy of any consecutive stationary block of length $`j`$. Then
```math
H(X_0\mid X_{-1},\ldots,X_{-n})=a_{n+1}-a_n
=H(X_0\mid X_1,\ldots,X_n).
```
Both the joint block and its conditioning block have the same length on each side, and stationarity makes their entropies equal after translation. No time-reversibility assumption is used. The argument is for finite block entropies, as otherwise these differences may be undefined.

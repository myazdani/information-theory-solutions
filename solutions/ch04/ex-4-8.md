# Exercise 4.8: Entropy per unit symbol duration

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 92. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Let $`p=p_1`$ and $`1-p=p_2`$. The mean duration is $`2-p`$, so the desired rate is $`h_2(p)/(2-p)`$. Its derivative has the sign of
```math
(2-p)h_2'(p)+h_2(p)=\log_2\frac{1-p}{p^2}.
```
The unique interior maximum solves $`1-p=p^2`$ and is $`p_1=(\sqrt5-1)/2=\phi^{-1}`$, with $`p_2=\phi^{-2}`$. Since $`-\log_2p_i`$ equals the duration of symbol $`i`$ times $`\log_2\phi`$, its entropy divided by its mean duration is exactly $`\log_2\phi\approx0.6942419136`$ bits per unit time. Both endpoint rates are zero.

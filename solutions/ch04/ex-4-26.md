# Exercise 4.26: Recording transitions instead of states

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 98. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

For $`Y_i=(X_{i-1},X_i)`$, the edge process is Markov, with transitions
```math
\Pr(Y_{i+1}=(b,c)\mid Y_i=(a,b))=P_{bc}.
```
Its stationary law on directed edges is $`\nu_{ab}=\mu_aP_{ab}`$. Therefore
```math
h(Y)=\sum_{a,b}\mu_aP_{ab}H(P_{b,\cdot})
=\sum_b\mu_bH(P_{b,\cdot})=h(X).
```
Equivalently, a block of consecutive edges determines and is determined by the corresponding state block plus its first state, so recording overlaps does not create a positive extra entropy rate. The placeholder for the first edge in the question is a finite boundary convention and does not affect the limit.

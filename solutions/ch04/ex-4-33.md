# Exercise 4.33: A four-state-chain information inequality

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 100. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

The Markov property and chain rule imply
```math
I(X_2;X_3)-I(X_1;X_3)=I(X_2;X_3\mid X_1),
I(X_2;X_4)-I(X_1;X_4)=I(X_2;X_4\mid X_1).
```
Conditional on $`X_1`$, the variables $`X_2\to X_3\to X_4`$ still form a Markov chain. Conditional data processing therefore orders the two right-hand sides. Rearranging yields the required inequality. In fact its right-minus-left gap is
```math
I(X_2;X_3\mid X_1,X_4)\ge0,
```
by expanding $`I(X_2;X_3,X_4\mid X_1)`$ in the two orders.

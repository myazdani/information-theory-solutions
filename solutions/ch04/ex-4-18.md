# Exercise 4.18: A fixed hidden coin

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 96. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Let $`h_2`$ denote binary entropy and put $`r=2p(1-p)`$.

## (a)
Given the chosen coin $`X`$, tosses are independent, so $`I(Y_1;Y_2\mid X)=0`$.

## (b)
Marginally $`Y_1`$ is fair. Given its value, the other toss differs with probability $`r`$, so $`H(Y_1,Y_2)=1+h_2(r)`$. Given the coin, the two-toss entropy is $`2h_2(p)`$. Hence
```math
I(X;Y_1,Y_2)=1+h_2(2p(1-p))-2h_2(p).
```

## (c)
The joint block entropy is $`H(X,Y_1^n)=1+nh_2(p)`$. Since $`0\le H(X\mid Y_1^n)\le1`$,
```math
nh_2(p)\le H(Y_1^n)\le1+nh_2(p).
```
After division by $`n`$, the process entropy rate is $`h_2(p)`$. At $`p=1/2`$, the coin identity has no observable effect and the rate is 1, agreeing with an iid fair process. When the two biases differ, their stationary mixture is nonergodic.

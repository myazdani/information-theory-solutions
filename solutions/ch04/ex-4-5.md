# Exercise 4.5: Entropy of recursively split trees

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 89–91. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Treat the tree as an ordered rooted binary tree, with distinguishable left and right subtrees, as in the diagrams. Write $`H_n=H(T_n)`$ and set $`H_1=0`$.

## (a)
The leaf count $`N_1`$ of the designated subtree is determined by the complete tree. Adding it does not change entropy.

## (b)
Apply the joint-entropy chain rule.

## (c)
By the second construction, $`N_1`$ is uniform on $`\{1,\ldots,n-1\}`$, hence has entropy $`\log_2(n-1)`$.

## (d)
Conditionally on $`N_1=k`$, the two ordered subtrees are independent and have laws $`T_k`$ and $`T_{n-k}`$. They determine the full tree. Thus the conditional entropy is $`H_k+H_{n-k}`$; average over $`k`$.

## (e)
As $`k`$ ranges from 1 to $`n-1`$, so does $`n-k`$. The two sums coincide, giving
```math
H_n=\log_2(n-1)+\frac2{n-1}\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}H_k.
```

## (f)
Multiply the recurrence at $`n`$ by $`n-1`$ and that at $`n-1`$ by $`n-2`$, then subtract. For $`n\ge3`$,
```math
(n-1)H_n=nH_{n-1}+(n-1)\log_2(n-1)-(n-2)\log_2(n-2).
```
Therefore
```math
\frac{H_n}{n}=\frac{H_{n-1}}{n-1}+c_n,\qquad
c_n=\frac{(n-1)\log_2(n-1)-(n-2)\log_2(n-2)}{n(n-1)}.
```
The numerator is nonnegative and, by the mean-value theorem for $`x\log_2x`$, is $`O(\log n)`$. Thus $`\sum_{n\ge3}c_n<\infty`$. Since $`H_2=0`$,
```math
\frac{H_n}{n}\longrightarrow c=\sum_{j=3}^\infty c_j
=2\sum_{m=2}^\infty\frac{\log_2m}{(m+1)(m+2)}\in(0,\infty).
```
The binary optimal expected description length lies between $`H_n`$ and $`H_n+1`$, so it has the same linear growth rate.

# Exercise 4.32: Equal uncertainty at matching time distances

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 100. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

An appropriate index is $`k=n+1`$. Conditional independence of past and future given the present gives
```math
H(X_{-n}\mid X_0,X_1)=H(X_{-n}\mid X_0),
\qquad H(X_{n+1}\mid X_0,X_1)=H(X_{n+1}\mid X_1).
```
Stationarity makes the second right-hand side $`H(X_n\mid X_0)`$. For the first, stationarity and equal one-state marginals give
```math
H(X_{-n}\mid X_0)=H(X_0\mid X_n)
=H(X_0,X_n)-H(X_n)=H(X_n\mid X_0).
```
Thus the two requested conditional entropies agree. This identifies a guaranteed index; special chains may have additional equalities.

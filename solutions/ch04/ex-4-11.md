# Exercise 4.11: Which stationary-process claims hold

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 93. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Assume finite entropies for the comparisons.

## (a)
True. Stationarity and symmetry of joint entropy give
```math
H(X_n\mid X_0)=H(X_n,X_0)-H(X_0)
=H(X_0,X_{-n})-H(X_0)=H(X_{-n}\mid X_0).
```

## (b)
False. Let $`A,B`$ be independent fair bits and define a bi-infinite process by $`X_{2j}=A`$, $`X_{2j+1}=B`$. Swapping $`A,B`$ leaves its distribution invariant, so it is stationary. But $`H(X_1\mid X_0)=1`$ and $`H(X_2\mid X_0)=0`$.

## (c)
True. Shift indices by $`-n`$ to rewrite the quantity as
```math
H(X_0\mid X_{1-n},\ldots,X_{-1},X_1).
```
Increasing $`n`$ adds a conditioning variable and therefore cannot increase entropy.

## (d)
True. The same translation yields
```math
H(X_0\mid X_{1-n},\ldots,X_{-1},X_1,\ldots,X_n).
```
Increasing $`n`$ adds one conditioning variable at each end; conditional entropy is nonincreasing.

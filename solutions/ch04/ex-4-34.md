# Exercise 4.34: An information bound for two downstream outputs

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 100. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

A chain-rule identity gives
```math
I(X;Z)+I(X;W)
=I(X;Z,W)+I(Z;W)-I(Z;W\mid X).
```
The last conditional information is nonnegative. Also the stipulated chain $`X\to Y\to(Z,W)`$ gives $`I(X;Z,W)\le I(X;Y)`$ by data processing. Combining the two inequalities proves
```math
I(X;Z)+I(X;W)\le I(X;Y)+I(Z;W).
```
The dependence term $`I(Z;W)`$ accounts for information the two downstream outputs may share.

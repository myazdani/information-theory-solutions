# Exercise 4.23: When the entropy rate equals one-letter entropy

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 97. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Assume finite one-symbol entropy.

## (a)
By stationarity and subadditivity,
```math
H(X_1^n)\le\sum_{i=1}^nH(X_i)=nH(X_1).
```
Divide by $`n`$ and take the limit to obtain $`h(X)\le H(X_1)`$.

## (b)
The conditional entropies $`h_i=H(X_i\mid X_1^{i-1})`$ form a nonincreasing sequence, with first value $`H(X_1)`$ and limit $`h(X)`$. If the limit equals the first value, every term must equal it. Thus $`I(X_i;X_1^{i-1})=0`$ for every $`i`$, and the joint law factors into its marginals at every block length. Stationarity makes those marginals identical: the process is iid. Conversely an iid process clearly has equality. This includes a deterministic iid source.

# Exercise 4.6: Entropy per symbol decreases

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 91. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Define $`h_i=H(X_i\mid X_1,\ldots,X_{i-1})`$. Conditioning and stationarity give
```math
h_{i+1}\le H(X_{i+1}\mid X_2,\ldots,X_i)=h_i.
```
The block entropy is $`H(X^n)=\sum_{i=1}^nh_i`$.

## (a)
Since $`h_n\le(h_1+\cdots+h_{n-1})/(n-1)`$, appending $`h_n`$ cannot increase the average. Hence $`H(X^n)/n\le H(X^{n-1})/(n-1)`$.

## (b)
Every one of $`h_1,\ldots,h_n`$ is at least $`h_n`$, so their average is at least $`h_n=H(X_n\mid X^{n-1})`$.

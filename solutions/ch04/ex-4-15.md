# Exercise 4.15: A fixed finite past has no rate cost

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 94. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Let $`a_m`$ be the entropy of a stationary block of length $`m`$, with finite one-symbol entropy. The two blocks in the question adjoin, so the chain rule and stationarity give
```math
H(X_1^n\mid X_{-k}^0)=a_{n+k+1}-a_{k+1}.
```
For fixed $`k`$, divide by $`n`$:
```math
\frac1nH(X_1^n\mid X_{-k}^0)
=\frac{n+k+1}{n}\frac{a_{n+k+1}}{n+k+1}-\frac{a_{k+1}}n\longrightarrow h(X).
```
Ordering the variables in the conditioned block in reverse does not change its entropy.

# Exercise 4.22: A walk through a cubic maze

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 97. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
A room's degree equals 3 plus the number of its coordinates that are in the middle layer. Thus there are 8 degree-3 corner rooms, 12 degree-4 edge-interior rooms, 6 degree-5 face-center rooms, and one degree-6 central room. Total degree is
```math
8\cdot3+12\cdot4+6\cdot5+1\cdot6=108.
```
The connected undirected walk has stationary mass $`d(v)/108`$ at each room: respectively $`1/36,1/27,5/108,1/18`$ for an individual room of those four types.

## (b)
The next-room entropy at degree $`d`$ is $`\log_2d`$. Averaging gives
```math
h=\frac{24\log_23+48\log_24+30\log_25+6\log_26}{108}
=\frac{24\log_23+96+30\log_25+6\log_26}{108}.
```

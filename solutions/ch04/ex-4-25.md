# Exercise 4.25: Information increases with more observations

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 97–98. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
The chain rule gives
```math
I(X;Y_1^{n+1})=I(X;Y_1^n)+I(X;Y_{n+1}\mid Y_1^n)\ge I(X;Y_1^n).
```

## (b)
For finite mutual informations, the sequence is constant for all $`n\ge1`$ exactly when every added term vanishes:
```math
I(X;Y_{n+1}\mid Y_1^n)=0\quad\text{for every }n\ge1.
```
Equivalently, for each $`n`$, $`X`$ and $`(Y_2,\ldots,Y_n)`$ are conditionally independent given $`Y_1`$: the first observation is sufficient for all information about $`X`$ contained in subsequent finite blocks. Marginal independence of $`X`$ from each later observation alone would not suffice.

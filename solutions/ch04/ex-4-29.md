# Exercise 4.29: A process of successive head times

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 99. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Let $`X_i=S_i-S_{i-1}`$ be the iid positive geometric gaps. Each has mean 2 and entropy 2 bits.

## (a)
The process is not stationary. For example $`S_1`$ can equal 1 while $`S_2`$ cannot, and $`\mathbb ES_n=2n`$ changes with time.

## (b)
The vector of partial sums and the vector of gaps are bijective by taking differences. Hence
```math
H(S_1,\ldots,S_n)=H(X_1,\ldots,X_n)=2n.
```

## (c)
Yes. Its block-entropy rate exists and equals $`\lim_nH(S_1^n)/n=2`$. Also $`H(S_n\mid S_1^{n-1})=H(X_n)=2`$. Stationarity is a sufficient condition for entropy-rate existence, not a necessary one.

## (d)
A direct generator tosses the fair coin until its $`n`$th head and returns the elapsed count. It uses $`S_n`$ tosses and therefore has expected cost $`2n`$ fair flips. This supplies the requested same-distribution generator. It is not a claim of minimum randomness among all algorithms that output only the count: a more economical sampler could avoid retaining the entire toss path. Any exact decision-tree sampler has expected length at least $`H(S_n)`$, whereas the direct construction spends $`H(S_1,\ldots,S_n)=2n`$ bits to generate all the gaps as well.

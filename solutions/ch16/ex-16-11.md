# Exercise 16.11: The first three finite-horizon universality factors

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 655.

## Solution

Use the continuous convention $`0^0=1`$ in the endpoint terms. It is convenient to calculate the reciprocal first:

```math
V_1^{-1}=1+1=2,
```

```math
V_2^{-1}=1+2(1/2)(1/2)+1=\frac52,
```

and

```math
V_3^{-1}=1+3(1/3)(2/3)^2
+3(2/3)^2(1/3)+1
=2+\frac49+\frac49=\frac{26}{9}.
```

Therefore

```math
\boxed{V_1=\frac12,\qquad V_2=\frac25,\qquad V_3=\frac9{26}.}
```

The corresponding decimals are 0.5, 0.4, and approximately 0.34615384615384615. The companion calculation evaluates the original finite sums using rational arithmetic rather than relying on rounded powers.

# Exercise 7.5 — Capacity of a product channel

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 224.

## Solution

For every possibly dependent input pair, conditional independence of the two component channels gives
```math
I(X_1,X_2;Y_1,Y_2)
=H(Y_1,Y_2)-H(Y_1\mid X_1)-H(Y_2\mid X_2)
\le I(X_1;Y_1)+I(X_2;Y_2)\le C_1+C_2.
```
Take $`X_1,X_2`$ independent, each with a capacity-achieving marginal. The outputs are then independent, making the first inequality equality, and the second is equality by the marginal choices. The capacity is therefore $`C_1+C_2`$ bits per simultaneous pair of transmissions.

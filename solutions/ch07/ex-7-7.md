# Exercise 7.7 — A cascade of independent binary flips

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 225.

## Solution

Represent each stage as addition modulo 2 of an independent $`Z_i\sim\operatorname{Bern}(p)`$. The overall error is their parity $`E_n=Z_1\oplus\cdots\oplus Z_n`$. Its signed expectation factorizes:
```math
\mathbb E(-1)^{E_n}=\prod_i\mathbb E(-1)^{Z_i}=(1-2p)^n.
```
Since this expectation equals $`1-2\Pr(E_n=1)`$, the cascade is a BSC with crossover
```math
p_n=\frac{1-(1-2p)^n}{2}.
```
If $`0<p<1`$, then $`p_n\to1/2`$, and its capacity $`1-h_2(p_n)\to0`$. For every initial input distribution, $`0\le I(X_0;X_n)\le1-h_2(p_n)`$, so its mutual information tends to zero as well. At $`p=0`$ or 1 the cascade is invertible and retains capacity one.

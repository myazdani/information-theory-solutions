# Exercise 7.11 — A time-varying product channel

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 226.

## Solution

Let $`C_i=\max_{p(x_i)}I(X_i;Y_i)`$ for the channel at time $`i`$. For any joint input law, conditional independence and memorylessness give
```math
I(X^n;Y^n)=H(Y^n)-\sum_iH(Y_i\mid X_i)
\le\sum_i I(X_i;Y_i)\le\sum_iC_i.
```
Choose the inputs independently with capacity-achieving marginals for their respective channels. The outputs are then independent and every inequality becomes equality. Thus $`\max_{p(x^n)}I(X^n;Y^n)=\sum_iC_i`$. For the binary symmetric channels drawn in the exercise, this becomes
```math
\sum_{i=1}^n[1-h_2(p_i)],
```
and independent fair input bits attain it.

# Exercise 7.9 — Uniform random codebooks on the one-sided channel

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 225.

## Solution

For fair input, the output-1 probability is $`1/4`$ and the average row entropy is $`1/2`$. Thus
```math
I_{\mathrm{uniform}}(X;Y)=h_2(1/4)-1/2\approx0.3112781245.
```
The iid random-coding theorem gives vanishing ensemble-average error for every rate strictly below this value, using a suitable decoder. It is therefore the supremum of achievable rates for this ensemble.

To see why higher rates cannot work, let $`\mathcal C`$ be the random codebook, $`M`$ the uniform message, and apply Fano conditionally on $`\mathcal C`$. Memorylessness and output-entropy subadditivity give
```math
I(M;Y^n\mid\mathcal C)\le
\sum_t\mathbb E_{\mathcal C}H(Y_t\mid\mathcal C)-n/2
\le n[h_2(1/4)-1/2],
```
where the final inequality uses entropy concavity and the ensemble's fair input marginals. Vanishing average error would force $`nR\le I(M;Y^n\mid\mathcal C)+o(n)`$, proving the rate bound. No assertion of vanishing error exactly at the boundary is needed.

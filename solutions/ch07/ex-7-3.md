# Exercise 7.3 — Dependent additive noise and block mutual information

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 224.

## Solution

Take $`X^n`$ uniform on $`\{0,1\}^n`$, independently of the noise. For every $`y^n`$,
```math
\Pr(Y^n=y^n)=\sum_{z^n}\Pr(Z^n=z^n)2^{-n}=2^{-n}.
```
Also, for each input vector the mapping from noise to output is a bijection, so $`H(Y^n\mid X^n)=H(Z^n)`$. Hence
```math
I(X^n;Y^n)=n-H(Z^n)\ge n-\sum_iH(Z_i)
=n[1-h_2(p)]=nC.
```
This input is actually optimal for the $`n`$-letter channel, since no output entropy can exceed $`n`$. Thus its block capacity is exactly $`n-H(Z^n)`$. Strict improvement over the independent-noise benchmark occurs when the noise coordinates have strictly less joint entropy than the sum of their marginal entropies.

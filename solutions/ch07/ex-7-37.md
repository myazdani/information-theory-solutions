# Exercise 7.37 — Independent draws landing in a jointly typical triple set

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 240.

## Solution

Let $`P=p_{XYZ}^{\otimes n}`$ and $`Q=p_X^{\otimes n}p_Y^{\otimes n}p_Z^{\otimes n}`$. Put
```math
T=H(X)+H(Y)+H(Z)-H(X,Y,Z)\ge0.
```
On the joint typical set $`A_\epsilon^{(n)}`$, the three marginal log-probabilities and the triple log-probability lie within their specified tolerances. Therefore
```math
2^{-n(T+4\epsilon)}\le\frac{Q(x^n,y^n,z^n)}{P(x^n,y^n,z^n)}
\le2^{-n(T-4\epsilon)}.
```
Multiply by $`P`$ and sum over the typical set to obtain the finite-block bounds
```math
P(A_\epsilon^{(n)})2^{-n(T+4\epsilon)}
\le Q(A_\epsilon^{(n)})
\le P(A_\epsilon^{(n)})2^{-n(T-4\epsilon)}.
```
For every $`\delta>0`$ and sufficiently large $`n`$, the iid laws of large numbers for the seven information quantities imply $`P(A_\epsilon^{(n)})\ge1-\delta`$. In particular,
```math
(1-\delta)2^{-n(T+4\epsilon)}
\le\Pr((\widetilde X^n,\widetilde Y^n,\widetilde Z^n)\in A_\epsilon^{(n)})
\le\min\{1,2^{-n(T-4\epsilon)}\}.
```
The exponent may also be written $`I(X;Y)+I((X,Y);Z)`$, or either cyclic analogue. Thus the pairwise entropies used in the definition impose additional typicality conditions but do not add separate terms to the leading exponent. Finite alphabets, or finiteness of the listed entropies with applicable laws of large numbers, justify the argument.

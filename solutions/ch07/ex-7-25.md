# Exercise 7.25 — Capacity through a finite bottleneck

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 234.

## Solution

For every input law, $`X\to V\to Y`$ gives
```math
I(X;Y)\le I(X;V)\le H(V)\le\log_2 k.
```
Taking the supremum over input laws proves $`C\le\log_2 k`$. More precisely $`C\le\min(\log_2 m,\log_2 k)`$. The bound is sharp when enough input/output symbols are available and the intermediate channel transmits a $`k`$-symbol choice without noise.

# Exercise 7.13 — A binary channel with erasures and flips

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 226–227.

## Solution

Assume $`0\le\alpha\le1`$ and $`0\le\epsilon\le1-\alpha`$. The erasure indicator is independent of the input. On the nonerasure event, the channel is a BSC with crossover $`q=\epsilon/(1-\alpha)`$.

### (a)
For any input law,
```math
I(X;Y)=(1-\alpha)I(X;Y\mid Y\ne e).
```
A fair input maximizes the conditional BSC information, so
```math
C=(1-\alpha)\left[1-h_2\!\left(\frac{\epsilon}{1-\alpha}\right)\right].
```
When $`\alpha=1`$ the capacity is zero, defined separately to avoid a zero denominator.

### (b)
At $`\alpha=0`$, the expression is $`1-h_2(\epsilon)`$, the BSC capacity.

### (c)
At $`\epsilon=0`$, the expression is $`1-\alpha`$, the binary erasure capacity. A conditional flip probability above $`1/2`$ causes no difficulty: complementing the nonerased output gives an equivalent BSC.

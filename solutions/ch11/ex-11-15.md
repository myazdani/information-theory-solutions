# Exercise 11.15 — A frequency ratio among six equally likely states

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 404.

## Solution

Let $`g(s_1)=1`$, $`g(s_2)=-2`$, and $`g=0`$ on the other states. The event is the exact type constraint $`\sum_xP(x)g(x)=0`$. Since the reference is uniform, its information projection maximizes entropy subject to this equality.

The Lagrange form is $`P^*(x)=e^{\theta g(x)}/Z`$. The relation $`P^*(s_1)=2P^*(s_2)`$ requires $`e^{3\theta}=2`$. Put $`a=2^{1/3}`$ and $`Z=4+a+a^{-2}=4+3\,2^{-2/3}`$. Then
```math
P^*=\frac1Z(a,a^{-2},1,1,1,1).
```
### (a)
Because $`\mathbb E_{P^*}g=0`$,
```math
D_2(P^*\|U_6)=\log_2(6/Z),\qquad H(P^*)=\log_2 Z.
```
The probability of the exact frequency event is $`2^{-n(\log_2(6/Z)+o(1))}`$. The type-count upper bound and a feasible rounded type with $`N_1=2N_2`$ give matching exponents; the four unconstrained counts absorb rounding, so no asymptotically empty subsequence arises.

### (b)
Strict concavity gives a unique maximizing type distribution, and types a fixed distance from it have strictly smaller entropy. Conditional types therefore concentrate at $`P^*`$. Exchangeability gives $`\Pr(X_1=s_i\mid\text{event})=\mathbb E[P_{X^n}(s_i)\mid\text{event}]\to P^*(s_i)`$. Thus the displayed six masses are the limiting first-atom distribution.

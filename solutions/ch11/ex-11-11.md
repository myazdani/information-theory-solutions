# Exercise 11.11 — A biased Cramér–Rao bound

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 402.

## Solution

Assume a common-support differentiable model for which derivatives pass through expectations and the score $`S_\theta=\partial_\theta\ln f(X;\theta)`$ has finite second moment. Normalization gives $`\mathbb ES_\theta=0`$. Since $`\mathbb E_\theta T=\theta+b_T(\theta)`$,
```math
\mathbb E_\theta[(T-\mathbb ET)S_\theta]
=\frac{d}{d\theta}\mathbb E_\theta T
=1+b_T'(\theta).
```
Cauchy–Schwarz now gives $`[1+b_T'(\theta)]^2\le\mathrm{Var}_\theta(T)J(\theta)`$. Finally,
```math
\mathbb E_\theta(T-\theta)^2
=\mathrm{Var}_\theta(T)+b_T(\theta)^2
\ge\frac{[1+b_T'(\theta)]^2}{J(\theta)}+b_T(\theta)^2.
```
This assumes $`J(\theta)>0`$; zero-information models need separate interpretation. An unbiased estimator has $`b_T\equiv0`$ and recovers the ordinary Cramér–Rao bound.

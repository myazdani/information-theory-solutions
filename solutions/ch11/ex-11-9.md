# Exercise 11.9 — Additivity of Fisher information

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 402.

## Solution

Let $`s_\theta(x)=\partial_\theta\ln f_\theta(x)`$. For two conditionally independent observations the joint score is $`s_\theta(X_1)+s_\theta(X_2)`$. Under the standard common-support differentiation assumptions, $`\mathbb E_\theta s_\theta(X_i)=\partial_\theta\int f_\theta=0`$. Therefore
```math
J_g(\theta)=\mathbb E[s_\theta(X_1)+s_\theta(X_2)]^2
=J_f(\theta)+J_f(\theta)+2\mathbb E s_\theta(X_1)\mathbb E s_\theta(X_2)
=2J_f(\theta).
```
The same proof gives $`nJ_f(\theta)`$ for $`n`$ independent observations. The zero-mean score property is the step that eliminates the cross term.

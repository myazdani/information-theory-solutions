# Exercise 11.8 — Fisher information for variance and exponential rate

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 401–402.

## Solution

The parameter $`\theta>0`$ is the variance in part (a) and the rate in part (b). Fisher information uses the natural-log score.

### (a)
For $`X\sim N(0,\theta)`$,
```math
\partial_\theta\ln f_\theta(X)=-\frac1{2\theta}+\frac{X^2}{2\theta^2}.
```
Using $`\mathrm{Var}(X^2)=2\theta^2`$ gives $`J(\theta)=1/(2\theta^2)`$.

### (b)
For exponential rate $`\theta`$, the score is $`1/\theta-X`$. Its mean is zero and its variance is $`1/\theta^2`$, so $`J(\theta)=1/\theta^2`$.

### (c)
The one-observation Cramér–Rao bounds for unbiased estimators are respectively $`2\theta^2`$ and $`\theta^2`$. With $`n`$ independent observations they become $`2\theta^2/n`$ and $`\theta^2/n`$. The first one-observation bound is attained by $`X^2`$. The second is only a lower bound; attaining it with an estimator of the exponential rate is not asserted.

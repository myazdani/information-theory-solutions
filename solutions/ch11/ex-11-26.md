# Exercise 11.26 — Exact conditional probabilities from exchangeability

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 407.

## Solution

### (a)
Conditional on $`\sum_iX_i=n/4`$, exchangeability makes all coordinates have the same conditional expectation. Their sum is fixed, so
```math
\Pr(X_1=1\mid\bar X_n=1/4)=\mathbb E[X_1\mid\text{event}]
=\frac1n\mathbb E\left[\sum_iX_i\mid\text{event}\right]=\frac14.
```
This is exact for every positive multiple of 4; the original Bernoulli success probability cancels. Equivalently, conditional strings with exactly $`n/4`$ ones are uniform.

### (b)
The constraint $`n^{-1}\sum_iX_i^2=1/2`$ says exactly half the coordinates are nonzero. By exchangeability the first coordinate is nonzero with conditional probability $`1/2`$. Symmetry between $`+1`$ and $`-1`$ remains valid under this squared constraint, so each has conditional probability $`1/4`$. Thus
```math
\Pr(X_1=+1\mid n^{-1}\sum_iX_i^2=1/2)=1/4
```
for every positive even $`n`$, and the requested limit is also $`1/4`$.

# Exercise 15.24: Three cumulative binary sums

Source: Cover and Thomas, 2nd ed. (2006), p. 605, Exercise 15.24. All sums are ordinary integer sums. Rates are bits per i.i.d. source triple.

## Solution

Let $`q=1-p`$, $`h=h_2(p)`$, $`a=2pq`$, and

```math
B_3=H(q^3,3pq^2,3p^2q,p^3).
```

The transformation is invertible because $`Z_1=X_1`$, $`Z_2=X_2-X_1`$, and $`Z_3=X_3-X_2`$. Hence the joint entropy is $`3h`$. The three-source Slepian–Wolf theorem requires, for every nonempty subset $`S`$,

```math
\sum_{i\in S}R_i\ge H(X_S\mid X_{S^c}).
```

Evaluating all seven inequalities gives the complete region

```math
R_1\ge a,\qquad R_2\ge a,\qquad R_3\ge h,
```

```math
R_1+R_2\ge3h-B_3,\qquad
R_1+R_3\ge h+a,\qquad
R_2+R_3\ge2h,
```

```math
R_1+R_2+R_3\ge3h.
```

Here are the conditional-entropy calculations. Given $`X_2`$, the only ambiguity in $`Z_1`$ occurs when $`Z_1+Z_2=1`$, an event of probability $`2pq`$; its two alternatives are equiprobable. Knowing $`X_3`$ as well only reveals independent $`Z_3`$. Thus $`H(X_1\mid X_2,X_3)=a`$. Given $`X_1,X_3`$, the uncertainty in $`X_2`$ is the uncertainty in $`Z_2`$ given $`Z_2+Z_3`$, also $`a`$. Given $`X_1,X_2`$, the remaining uncertainty in $`X_3`$ is that of independent $`Z_3`$, namely $`h`$.

For pair constraints, subtract the complementary marginal entropy from $`3h`$. The complementary entropies are $`H(X_3)=B_3`$, $`H(X_2)=2h-a`$ (the binary-pair entropy minus its one-bit ambiguity on the sum-one event), and $`H(X_1)=h`$. These give exactly the three displayed pair bounds.

The theorem provides both necessity and asymptotic achievability; weak inequalities denote closure. At $`p=0`$ or $`p=1`$, all sources are deterministic and all right-hand sides vanish, as they should.

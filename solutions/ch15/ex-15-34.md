# Exercise 15.34: Product and sum of two independent bits

Source: Cover and Thomas, 2nd ed. (2006), p. 608, Exercise 15.34. Let $`q=1-p`$. Part (b) prints $`(X^n,Y^n)`$ although those variables are not defined in the exercise; below it is interpreted as the original pair $`(Z_1^n,Z_2^n)`$. The residual uncertainty in the recovered pair $`(U^n,V^n)`$ itself tends to zero per symbol.

## Solution

### (a) Slepian–Wolf region

The possible $`(U,V)`$ pairs are $`(0,0),(0,1),(1,2)`$, with probabilities $`q^2,2pq,p^2`$. In particular $`U=\mathbf1\{V=2\}`$, so $`H(U\mid V)=0`$. Set

```math
J=H(q^2,2pq,p^2).
```

Then $`H(U,V)=H(V)=J`$ and $`H(U)=h_2(p^2)`$. The exact closed region is

```math
R_1\ge0,\qquad R_2\ge J-h_2(p^2),\qquad R_1+R_2\ge J.
```

The Slepian–Wolf theorem gives necessity and achievability. Equivalently the second lower bound is $`(1-p^2)h_2(2pq/(1-p^2))`$, interpreted as zero when its prefactor vanishes.

### (b) Residual uncertainty about the original bits

The product and sum distinguish the original pairs $`(0,0)`$ and $`(1,1)`$. They merge $`(0,1)`$ and $`(1,0)`$, which are equiprobable and together have probability $`2pq`$. Consequently

```math
H(Z_1,Z_2\mid U,V)=2pq
```

bits per source pair, and by the i.i.d. product law

```math
H(Z_1^n,Z_2^n\mid U^n,V^n)=2npq.
```

This is information irreversibly discarded by the product/sum transformation; it remains even after perfect recovery of both compressed sources. It also verifies $`J=2h_2(p)-2pq`$ by the entropy chain rule.

If $`M`$ denotes the two deterministic compression indices of a reliable code, it is a function of $`(U^n,V^n)`$, and the exact chain-rule identity is

```math
H(Z_1^n,Z_2^n\mid M)=2npq+H(U^n,V^n\mid M).
```

Fano makes the last term $`o(n)`$ under vanishing recovery error, so the asymptotic residual per pair is still $`2pq`$. At $`p=0,1`$ there is no uncertainty; at $`p=1/2`$ the residual is one half bit per pair.

# Exercise 15.12: Axis intercepts of a degraded broadcast region

Source: Cover and Thomas, 2nd ed. (2006), p. 601, Exercise 15.12. The labels $`a,b`$ denote the horizontal and vertical intercepts in the printed diagram.

## Solution

### (a) The two single-user capacities

Suppressing one message reduces the channel to an ordinary point-to-point channel, so

```math
a=\max_{p(x)}I(X;Y_1),\qquad b=\max_{p(x)}I(X;Y_2).
```

These are both achievable with the unused message fixed, and no larger intercept is possible by the point-to-point converse. They also follow from the degraded broadcast formula: choose $`U`$ constant for the first intercept and $`U=X`$ for the second, each with an optimizing input law.

### (b) Ordering

For every fixed input law the Markov chain $`X\to Y_1\to Y_2`$ and data processing give $`I(X;Y_2)\le I(X;Y_1)`$. Maximizing proves $`b\le a`$. Equality is possible, for example if $`Y_2`$ retains all of $`Y_1`$'s information about a capacity-achieving input; strict inequality is not required.

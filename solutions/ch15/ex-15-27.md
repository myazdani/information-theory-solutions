# Exercise 15.27: A successive-decoding identity

Source: Cover and Thomas, 2nd ed. (2006), p. 606, Exercise 15.27. Assume $`P_1,P_2\ge0`$, $`N>0`$, and $`C(x)=\tfrac12\log_2(1+x)`$.

## Solution

Combine the logarithms and cancel the intermediate factor:

```math
C(P_1/N)+C(P_2/(P_1+N))
=\frac12\log_2\left[\frac{N+P_1}{N}
\frac{N+P_1+P_2}{N+P_1}\right]
=\frac12\log_2\frac{N+P_1+P_2}{N}
=C((P_1+P_2)/N).
```

Operationally, decode user 2 first while treating user 1's independent Gaussian signal as additional noise. This permits rate $`C(P_2/(P_1+N))`$. Subtract the decoded waveform and decode user 1 at rate $`C(P_1/N)`$. The sum equals the single-user rate with their pooled power. Rates exactly at the displayed values mean limits of reliable codes at slightly smaller rates. The identity also holds when either power is zero; the positive noise assumption keeps all denominators defined.

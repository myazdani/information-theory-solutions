# Exercise 5.9: Redundancy approaching one bit

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 144. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Take a binary source with probabilities $`(1-\delta,\delta)`$, where $`0<\delta<1/2`$. Both symbols must receive distinct nonempty codewords, and the optimal prefix code is 0,1, with mean length 1. Its entropy $`h_2(\delta)`$ tends to zero as $`\delta\downarrow0`$. Given any $`\epsilon>0`$, choose $`\delta`$ so small that $`h_2(\delta)<\epsilon`$. Then
```math
L=1>H(X)+1-\epsilon.
```
Using a genuinely positive $`\delta`$ avoids collapsing the alphabet to a single deterministic outcome.

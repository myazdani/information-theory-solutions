# Exercise 5.10: Equality in a ternary entropy bound

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 145. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Assume the listed $`m`$ outcomes all have positive probability.

## (a)
For lengths $`\ell_i`$, set $`K=\sum_i3^{-\ell_i}\le1`$ and $`q_i=3^{-\ell_i}/K`$. In base-3 units,
```math
L-H_3(p)=D_3(p\Vert q)-\log_3K\ge0.
```
Equality requires both $`K=1`$ and $`p=q`$. Hence every $`p_i=3^{-\ell_i}`$, the asserted reciprocal power of 3.

## (b)
Let $`L_{\max}`$ be the largest finite length and multiply Kraft equality by $`3^{L_{\max}}`$:
```math
\sum_{i=1}^m3^{L_{\max}-\ell_i}=3^{L_{\max}}.
```
Each term on the left is odd, while the right is odd; therefore $`m`$ is odd. Equivalently, a full finite ternary tree has $`m=1+2I`$ leaves. The singleton zero-length case also has odd $`m=1`$.

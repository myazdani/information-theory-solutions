# Exercise 5.26: Minimum aggregate merge volume

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 149. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Assume $`W_i\ge0`$ and total value $`W=\sum_iW_i>0`$.

## (a)
Every sequence of pairwise merges defines a full binary tree whose leaves are the original companies. A leaf's value is counted once at each merge above it, so the total reported volume is
```math
V=\sum_iW_i\ell_i=W\sum_i\widetilde W_i\ell_i,
\qquad\widetilde W_i=W_i/W,
```
where $`\ell_i`$ is its leaf depth. Conversely every such tree can be realized by merging sibling subcompanies bottom-up. Minimizing volume is therefore exactly minimizing a binary prefix-code mean length under $`\widetilde W`$. Repeatedly merging the two least values is Huffman's rule and is optimal.

## (b)
The source-coding bounds, multiplied by $`W`$, give
```math
WH(\widetilde W)\le V\le WH(\widetilde W)+W.
```
For strictly positive probabilities on at least two symbols the usual strict upper bound also applies. If total value is zero then every volume is zero; the normalized distribution is unnecessary.

# Exercise 5.15: A Huffman tree and its matching dyadic law

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 146. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
Merge the two 0.1 masses, then merge the resulting 0.2 with the original 0.2; merge the two 0.3 masses and finally the 0.4 and 0.6 nodes. One code is $`(10,11,00,010,011)`$, with lengths $`(2,2,2,3,3)`$. Its mean is
```math
L=2(0.3+0.3+0.2)+3(0.1+0.1)=2.2.
```

## (b)
Assign each codeword probability $`p'_i=2^{-\ell_i}`$, giving $`p'=(1/4,1/4,1/4,1/8,1/8)`$. Kraft equality makes this a probability vector, and $`-\log_2p'_i=\ell_i`$ gives $`H(p')=\sum_ip'_i\ell_i=9/4`$. Thus the same code achieves its entropy exactly.

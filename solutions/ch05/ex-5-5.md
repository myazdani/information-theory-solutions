# Exercise 5.5: One tree optimal for two distributions

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 143. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Scale the first distribution by 15, giving weights $`(5,3,3,2,2)`$. Binary Huffman merges are $`2+2=4`$, $`3+3=6`$, $`4+5=9`$, and $`6+9=15`$. One resulting code, in source order, is
```math
(11,00,01,100,101),\qquad\ell=(2,2,2,3,3),\qquad L=34/15.
```
For five equiprobable symbols, an optimal full binary tree has three leaves at depth 2 and two at depth 3; it is also obtained by Huffman merging five equal weights. Therefore this same code is optimal under the uniform distribution, with mean length $`12/5`$. The codes need not have equal redundancy under the two laws.

# Exercise 5.3: Kraft slack leaves undecodable strings

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 143. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Generate an infinite iid uniform sequence over the $`D`$ output symbols. For a prefix code, the events that its first symbols equal a particular codeword are disjoint. Their total probability is exactly $`K=\sum_iD^{-\ell_i}<1`$. Therefore there exists an infinite sequence having no codeword as its initial segment. Every nonempty finite prefix of that sequence fails to be a concatenation of codewords, since any such concatenation would begin with a codeword. Taking prefixes of unbounded lengths proves the claim. This proof works even for a countable prefix code and avoids assuming a largest codeword length.

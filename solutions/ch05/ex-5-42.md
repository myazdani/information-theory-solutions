# Exercise 5.42: Possible ternary Huffman depths

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 157. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Both listed alphabet sizes are odd, so a ternary Huffman construction needs no zero-weight padding and its finite tree must be full.

## (a)
The Kraft sum for $`(1,2,2,2,2)`$ is $`1/3+4/9=7/9<1`$. It cannot be a full ternary Huffman tree and therefore cannot be its length vector.

## (b)
The second list has eight lengths 2 and three lengths 3. Its Kraft sum is $`8/9+3/27=1`$, and it is achievable. Assign probabilities $`1/9`$ to the eight shorter leaves and $`1/27`$ to the three deeper ones. Huffman first merges the latter three into a ninth mass $`1/9`$, then merges triples of these equal masses to obtain three masses $`1/3`$, and finally merges the root. Thus exactly the specified depths result.

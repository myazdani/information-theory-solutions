# Exercise 5.6: Which listed trees could be Huffman?

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 143. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
Possible. The code is a full binary tree and is optimal for the dyadic probabilities $`(1/2,1/4,1/4)`$; Huffman produces it.

## (b)
Impossible for the standard binary Huffman construction. Its Kraft sum is $`3/4+1/8=7/8`$, whereas the binary Huffman tree for at least two symbols is full and has Kraft sum 1. The single-child branch can be shortened.

## (c)
Impossible. A two-symbol binary Huffman code uses the two one-bit words, whereas these two words both have length 2 and Kraft sum $`1/2`$. The conclusion concerns being a Huffman output; allowing degenerate zero probabilities can make non-Huffman redundant codes tie an objective, but does not turn their trees into Huffman constructions.

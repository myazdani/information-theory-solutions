# Exercise 5.13: How many possible objects?

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 145. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Let $`M`$ be the number of possible objects and $`L_*`$ the optimal mean number of binary questions. The source-coding bounds imply
```math
L_*\lt H(X)+1\le\log_2M+1.
```
With $`L_*=38.5`$, this gives $`\log_2M>37.5`$, hence $`M>2^{37.5}`$, approximately $`1.94\times10^{11}`$ objects. This is a lower bound on the universe's supported size; nonuniformity can make its actual size much larger.

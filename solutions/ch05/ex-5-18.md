# Exercise 5.18: A nonprefix but uniquely decodable code

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 147. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
No. The word 0 is a proper prefix of 01, so decoding the first 0 immediately is not possible.

## (b)
Yes. Decoding from the right is unambiguous: a terminal 1 must be part of a final 01; a terminal 0 must be the final one-symbol word 0. Delete that word and repeat. Equivalently, the two codewords are suffix-free.

## (c)
Yes. The two source symbols have different codewords, so the encoding of a single symbol is injective.

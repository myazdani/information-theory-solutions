# Exercise 6.14 — Weighted geometric comparison of odds

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 180.

## Solution

For either positive odds schedule, proportional betting $`b_i=p_i`$ maximizes $`\sum_i p_i\log_2(b_i o_i)`$. Therefore
```math
W^*(o)-W^*(o')=\sum_i p_i\log_2(o_i/o_i').
```
The first odds schedule yields a strictly larger rate exactly when
```math
\prod_{i:p_i>0}o_i^{p_i}>\prod_{i:p_i>0}(o_i')^{p_i}.
```
This repeats the comparison in Exercise 6.9. Equal weighted geometric means give equal growth rates. The result assumes all wealth is bet; allowing a cash option with subfair schedules leads instead to the optimization in Exercise 6.2.

# Exercise 6.5 — Bets that beat public odds

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 177.

## Solution

## (a)
The entropy is $`H(p)=3/2`$ bits.

## (b)
For independent repeated races with a fixed allocation,
```math
W(b)=\tfrac12\log_2(4b_1)+\tfrac14\log_2(4b_2)+\tfrac14\log_2(2b_3)
=\tfrac14\log_2(128 b_1^2 b_2 b_3).
```
Consequently the desired set is
```math
\{b_i>0:\ b_1+b_2+b_3=1,\quad b_1^2b_2b_3>1/128\}.
```
Equivalently, $`D(p\|b)<D(p\|r)=1/4`$. Positive $`W`$ implies almost-sure divergence by the strong law; negative $`W`$ implies decay to zero. At $`W=0`$ there is no almost-sure divergence: either the return is identically one, or the centered finite-variance log-return random walk oscillates almost surely. A zero allocation has eventual ruin. The optimum $`b=p`$ lies in the displayed region and yields $`1/4`$ bit per race.

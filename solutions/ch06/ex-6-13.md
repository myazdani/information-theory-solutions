# Exercise 6.13 — A sure payoff versus optimal growth

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 180.

## Solution

### (a)
Equalizing the two possible gross returns yields $`10b=30(1-b)`$, so $`b=3/4`$. The terminal factor is $`15/2=7.5`$ regardless of the result. Its rate is $`\log_2(15/2)`$.

### (b)
The expected log return is $`\frac12\log_2(10b)+\frac12\log_2(30(1-b))`$. Maximizing $`b(1-b)`$ gives $`b^*=1/2`$ and
```math
W^*=\tfrac12\log_2 75.
```
Its excess over the sure-payoff strategy is
```math
\tfrac12\log_2 75-\log_2(15/2)=\tfrac12\log_2(4/3)>0.
```
The optimal-growth strategy sacrifices certainty in each race for a larger almost-sure long-run exponential growth rate.

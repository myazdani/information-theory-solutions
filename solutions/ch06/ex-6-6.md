# Exercise 6.6 — Decay under unit payouts

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 177–178.

## Solution

## (a)
Assuming $`b_i>0`$ whenever $`p_i>0`$, the strong law gives
```math
\frac1n\log_2(S_n/S_0)\longrightarrow\sum_i p_i\log_2 b_i
=-H(p)-D(p\|b).
```
A zero bet on a positive-probability outcome instead gives eventual ruin. Thus the signed growth exponent is the displayed expression; the positive decay exponent is its negative.

## (b)
The maximum signed exponent is $`-H(p)`$, attained by $`b^*=p`$ (with zeros outside the support).

## (c)
Entropy on three outcomes is at most $`\log_2 3`$, with equality only at the uniform distribution. Hence $`p=(1/3,1/3,1/3)`$ produces the fastest optimal decay, $`S_n/S_0=3^{-n}`$. If the race is deterministic there is no decay, an exception to the blanket decay claim in the question.

# Exercise 6.12 — Extremal winning distributions

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 180.

## Solution

Write $`r_i=1/o_i`$, $`R=\sum_i r_i`$, and $`q_i=r_i/R`$. The optimal fully invested growth rate is
```math
W^*(p)=\sum_i p_i\log_2(p_i o_i)=D(p\|q)-\log_2 R.
```
### (a)
Since $`\sum_i p_i\log_2 p_i\le0`$,
```math
W^*(p)\le\sum_i p_i\log_2 o_i\le\log_2\max_i o_i.
```
Equality is attained by a point mass on any horse having the largest odds. A nontrivial mixture of tied largest-odds horses has positive entropy and does not attain equality.

### (b)
Relative entropy is nonnegative, with equality precisely at $`p=q`$. Thus the minimizing distribution and value are
```math
p_i^*=\frac{1/o_i}{\sum_j1/o_j},\qquad
\min_p W^*(p)=-\log_2\sum_j\frac1{o_j}.
```
The odds are assumed finite and strictly positive.

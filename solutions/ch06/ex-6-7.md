# Exercise 6.7 — Twenty races at optimal proportions

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 178.

## Solution

For equal payout 4, the log-optimal fractions equal the winning probabilities. Initial bets are therefore $50, $25, $12.50, and $12.50, and the same fractions of current wealth are rebalanced before every race.

The entropy is $`H(p)=7/4`$ bits, so the optimal doubling rate is $`W^*=2-7/4=1/4`$ bit per race. The growth-rate estimate after 20 races is
```math
S_{20}\approx100\,2^{20/4}=\$3200.
```
This is a typical/geometric growth estimate, not a guaranteed outcome or arithmetic expectation. In fact $`\mathbb E\log_2(S_{20}/100)=5`$ exactly, whereas $`\mathbb E S_{20}=100(11/8)^{20}`$. Numerical evaluation is reproduced in `src/information_theory/ch06/compute.py`.

# Exercise 6.15 — Fair odds and binary side information

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 180.

## Solution

Assume positive winning probabilities; zero-probability outcomes can be omitted.

### (a)
For every admissible allocation,
```math
\mathbb ES=\sum_xp(x)b(x)/p(x)=\sum_x b(x)=1.
```
### (b)
The expected log return is $`W(b)=-D(p\|b)\le0`$. Its maximum is $`W^*=0`$, attained at $`b=p`$, which makes the wealth factor identically one.

### (c)
Given the observed partition indicator $`Y`$, bet according to $`p(x\mid Y)`$. Writing $`q=p_1+p_2`$, the return is $`1/q`$ when $`Y=1`$ and $`1/(1-q)`$ otherwise. The optimal rate increase is
```math
\Delta W=q\log_2(1/q)+(1-q)\log_2(1/(1-q))=h_2(q).
```
This also follows by applying the conditional relative-entropy optimality argument separately to the two groups.

### (d)
Because $`Y`$ is a function of $`X`$, $`H(Y\mid X)=0`$, and $`I(X;Y)=H(Y)=h_2(p_1+p_2)`$. The endpoint values use $`0\log 0=0`$.

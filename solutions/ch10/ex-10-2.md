# Exercise 10.2 — A forbidden false positive in binary reproduction

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 336.

## Solution

Finite expected distortion forbids $`\Pr(\widehat X=1\mid X=0)>0`$. Let $`q=\Pr(\widehat X=0\mid X=1)`$. The distortion is $`q/2`$, and
```math
I(X;\widehat X)=h_2((1-q)/2)-\tfrac12h_2(q).
```
Increasing $`q`$ degrades the reproduction channel (additional erasure of 1 into 0) and cannot increase mutual information. Hence the maximum permitted $`q`$ is optimal. For $`0\le D\le1/2`$,
```math
R(D)=h_2(1/2-D)-\tfrac12h_2(2D)
=1-(1/2+D)h_2\!\left(\frac{D}{1/2+D}\right).
```
For $`D\ge1/2`$, constant reproduction 0 has zero rate and distortion $`1/2`$, so $`R(D)=0`$. Negative distortion budgets are infeasible. The second expression follows by conditioning on the only ambiguous reconstruction, namely $`\widehat X=0`$.

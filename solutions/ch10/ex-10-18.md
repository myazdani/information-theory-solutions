# Exercise 10.18 — Affine changes to a distortion measure

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 344.

## Solution

### (a)
Since $`\mathbb E\widetilde d=\mathbb Ed+a`$, the feasible channels at new budget $`D`$ are exactly those at old budget $`D-a`$. Therefore $`\widetilde R(D)=R(D-a)`$, with infeasible budgets interpreted as positive infinity.

### (b)
If $`b>0`$, $`R^*(D)=R(D/b)`$. If $`b=0`$, the distortion is identically zero, so $`R^*(D)=0`$ for $`D\ge0`$ and there is no feasible channel for $`D<0`$. The zero case must be treated separately rather than dividing by $`b`$.

### (c)
For $`\sigma^2>0`$, an admissible budget above 3 corresponds to mean-square budget $`(D-3)/5`$. Thus
```math
R(D)=\begin{cases}
+\infty,&D\le3,\\
\frac12\log_2\frac{5\sigma^2}{D-3},&3\lt D\lt 3+5\sigma^2,\\
0,&D\ge3+5\sigma^2.
\end{cases}
```
For $`D<3`$ the value denotes infeasibility; at $`D=3`$ it denotes the infinite rate needed for exact continuous reconstruction. A degenerate Gaussian $`\sigma^2=0`$ instead has zero rate for every $`D\ge3`$.

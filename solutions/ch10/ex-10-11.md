# Exercise 10.11 — A fourth-moment Shannon lower bound

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 339.

## Solution

Let $`Z_0=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-x^4}\,dx=\Gamma(1/4)/2`$. Integration by parts gives the source fourth moment $`c=1/4`$. The maximum-entropy density under fourth-moment constraint $`a>0`$ is
```math
q_a(x)=\frac{\exp[-x^4/(4a)]}{Z_0(4a)^{1/4}}.
```
Indeed, nonnegativity of divergence to $`q_a`$ gives
```math
h_2(U)\le\log_2[Z_0(4a)^{1/4}]+\frac{\mathbb EU^4}{4a}\log_2e
\le\log_2[Z_0(4a)^{1/4}]+\tfrac14\log_2e,
```
and $`q_a`$ attains equality. This proves the maximum-entropy formula $`g(a)`$ and in particular $`h_2(X)=g(c)`$ for the source in the question.

For every test channel meeting $`\mathbb E(X-\widehat X)^4\le D`$,
```math
h_2(X\mid\widehat X)=h_2(X-\widehat X\mid\widehat X)
\le h_2(X-\widehat X)\le g(D).
```
Therefore $`I(X;\widehat X)\ge g(c)-g(D)`$, and minimizing proves the requested bound. Explicitly,
```math
R(D)\ge\max\left\{0,\frac14\log_2\frac{c}{D}\right\},\qquad c=1/4,
```
for $`D>0`$. For $`D\ge c`$, reproduction zero achieves zero rate. At zero distortion an exact continuous reconstruction requires unbounded rate.

# Exercise 10.3 — Evaluating an asymmetric binary test channel

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 337.

## Solution

Parameterize an arbitrary conditional reproduction law by
```math
\alpha=\Pr(\widehat X=1\mid X=0),\qquad
\beta=\Pr(\widehat X=0\mid X=1).
```
Since the source is fair, the output-1 probability is $`(1+\alpha-\beta)/2`$. Therefore the requested mutual information and distortion are
```math
I(X;\widehat X)=h_2\!\left(\frac{1+\alpha-\beta}{2}\right)
-\frac12h_2(\alpha)-\frac12h_2(\beta),
\qquad D=\frac{a\alpha+b\beta}{2}.
```
Consequently a complete two-variable characterization is
```math
R(D)=\min_{\substack{0\le\alpha,\beta\le1\\a\alpha+b\beta\le2D}}
\left[h_2\!\left(\frac{1+\alpha-\beta}{2}\right)
-\frac12h_2(\alpha)-\frac12h_2(\beta)\right].
```
For positive $`a,b`$, $`R(0)=1`$, while $`R(D)=0`$ for $`D\ge\min(a,b)/2`$, by always reproducing the cheaper constant symbol. Zero costs can create zero-distortion zero-rate cases. These formulas evaluate every fixed $`p(\widehat x\mid x)`$ without imposing the generally unjustified symmetry $`\alpha=\beta`$.

# Exercise 10.15 — Convexity and a distortion-rate converse

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 341.

## Solution

### (a)
$`D(R)`$ is nonincreasing: increasing $`R`$ enlarges the feasible set of test channels.

### (b)
It is convex. Mix two test channels with probabilities $`\theta,1-\theta`$. Distortion mixes linearly, while mutual information is convex in the test channel for a fixed source law. A mixture of channels feasible at $`R_1,R_2`$ is therefore feasible at $`\theta R_1+(1-\theta)R_2`$, giving
```math
D(\theta R_1+(1-\theta)R_2)
\le\theta D(R_1)+(1-\theta)D(R_2).
```
Approximation handles unattained infima.

### (c), with every labeled proof step
- (c.a), equation (10.162): block distortion is defined as the arithmetic mean of the letter distortions.
- (c.b), equation (10.163): interchange expectation and the finite sum by linearity.
- (c.c), equation (10.164): the actual joint law of $`(X_i,\widehat X_i)`$ is a feasible test channel at rate $`I(X_i;\widehat X_i)`$, so its distortion is at least the minimizing $`D`$ at that rate.
- (c.d), equation (10.165): Jensen's inequality for the convex function $`D(R)`$ moves the average inside.
- (c.e), equation (10.166): independence of the source coordinates and conditional entropy subadditivity give $`\sum_i I(X_i;\widehat X_i)\le I(X^n;\widehat X^n)`$. Since $`D`$ is nonincreasing, this increase in its argument can only decrease its value, producing the displayed direction.
- (c.f), equation (10.167): $`X^n\to J\to\widehat X^n`$ and $`|\mathcal J|\le2^{nR}`$ imply $`I(X^n;\widehat X^n)\le H(J)\le nR`$. Apply monotonicity once more to get $`D(n^{-1}I(X^n;\widehat X^n))\ge D(R)`$.

The starting equality identifies the code's actual expected distortion. The whole chain thus proves every rate-$`R`$ block code has distortion at least $`D(R)`$.

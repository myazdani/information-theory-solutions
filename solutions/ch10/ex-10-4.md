# Exercise 10.4 — Removing rowwise distortion offsets

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 337.

## Solution

For every conditional reproduction distribution,
```math
\mathbb E d'(X,\widehat X)=\mathbb E d(X,\widehat X)-\sum_i p_iw_i
=\mathbb E d(X,\widehat X)-w.
```
Thus the feasible test channels for the new budget $`D`$ are exactly those for the old budget $`D+w`$. The mutual-information objective is unchanged, proving
```math
R'(D)=R(D+w).
```
Choose $`w_i=\min_{\widehat x}d(i,\widehat x)`$ when these minima exist. Every new row then has minimum zero, and the original operational distortion is recovered by adding the fixed expected offset $`w`$. Only the origin of the distortion scale has changed. The identity also holds for infeasible budgets if the infimum over an empty set is defined as positive infinity.

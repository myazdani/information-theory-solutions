# Exercise 10.14 — Separate compression of independent sources

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 340–341.

## Solution

### (a)
Independence gives $`H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y)`$. For any admissible joint reproduction,
```math
H(X,Y\mid\widehat X,\widehat Y)
\le H(X\mid\widehat X)+H(Y\mid\widehat Y).
```
Thus
```math
I(X,Y;\widehat X,\widehat Y)
\ge I(X;\widehat X)+I(Y;\widehat Y)
\ge R_X(D_1)+R_Y(D_2).
```
Minimizing proves the requested bound.

### (b)
Use independent optimal test channels $`p(\widehat x\mid x)p(\widehat y\mid y)`$. The source independence makes $`(X,\widehat X)`$ independent of $`(Y,\widehat Y)`$, so the joint mutual information is exactly the sum of the individual mutual informations, while each distortion meets its own constraint. If minima are not attained, use arbitrarily close approximating channels. Consequently
```math
R_{X,Y}(D_1,D_2)=R_X(D_1)+R_Y(D_2).
```
There is no rate saving from joint compression of independent sources when both individual distortion constraints must be met.

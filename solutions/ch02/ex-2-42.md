# Exercise 2.42: Directions of basic entropy comparisons

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 52–53. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
Equality: multiplication by the nonzero constant 5 is injective, so $`H(5X)=H(X)`$.

## (b)
$`I(g(X);Y)\le I(X;Y)`$ by data processing for $`Y\to X\to g(X)`$.

## (c)
$`H(X_0\mid X_{-1})\ge H(X_0\mid X_{-1},X_1)`$, because conditioning reduces entropy.

## (d)
The ratio is at most 1 by joint-entropy subadditivity, provided its denominator is positive and finite. It equals 1 exactly under independence. If both marginal entropies are zero, the written ratio is $`0/0`$ and undefined; the underlying inequality still holds.

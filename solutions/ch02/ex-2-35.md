# Exercise 2.35: Two unequal relative entropies

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 51. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

The laws are $`p=(1/2,1/4,1/4)`$ and $`q=(1/3,1/3,1/3)`$. Direct calculation yields
```math
H(p)=\frac32,\quad H(q)=\log_2 3,
\quad D(p\Vert q)=\log_2 3-\frac32\approx0.084963,
\quad D(q\Vert p)=\frac53-\log_2 3\approx0.081704.
```
For the last expression the cross-entropy is $`(1+2+2)/3=5/3`$. The two divergences differ by $`2\log_2 3-19/6\approx0.003258`$, confirming that relative entropy is not symmetric.

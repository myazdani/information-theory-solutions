# Exercise 2.29: Four information inequalities

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 50. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Assume finite entropies so that the differences in the questions are defined.

## (a)
The gap is $`H(Y\mid X,Z)\ge0`$ by the conditional chain rule. Equality means that $`Y`$ is almost surely determined by $`(X,Z)`$.

## (b)
The chain rule gives a gap of $`I(Y;Z\mid X)\ge0`$. Equality means conditional independence of $`Y`$ and $`Z`$ given $`X`$.

## (c)
The assertion is $`H(Z\mid X,Y)\le H(Z\mid X)`$. Its gap is $`I(Z;Y\mid X)`$, so equality again means $`Z\perp Y\mid X`$.

## (d)
This claimed inequality is always an equality. Expand the same information in two orders:
```math
I(Z;X,Y)=I(Z;Y)+I(Z;X\mid Y)
=I(Z;X)+I(Z;Y\mid X).
```
Rearranging gives exactly the two sides in (d). No additional distributional condition is necessary.

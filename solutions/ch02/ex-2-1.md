# Exercise 2.1: Waiting for a head

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 43–44. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
The first head occurs at time $`n\ge1`$ with probability $`2^{-n}`$. Hence
```math
H(X)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty 2^{-n}\log_2(2^n)
=\sum_{n=1}^\infty n2^{-n}=2\ \text{bits}.
```
The series converges, so all displayed expectations are finite.

## (b)
Ask successively whether $`X=1`$, whether $`X=2`$, and so on, stopping at the first yes. Given that the first $`j-1`$ answers were no, the probability of yes at question $`j`$ is $`1/2`$. The number of questions is exactly $`X`$, with mean 2. The resulting binary decision tree is a prefix code with lengths $`n`$ and Kraft sum 1. Its mean length equals the entropy lower bound, so it is optimal among binary yes/no decision trees.

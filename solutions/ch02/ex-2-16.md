# Exercise 2.16: A finite bottleneck

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 46–47. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
Data processing and the entropy bound for a $`k`$-point alphabet imply
```math
I(X_1;X_3)\le I(X_1;X_2)\le H(X_2)\le\log_2k.
```
Neither stationarity nor equal endpoint alphabet sizes are needed.

## (b)
For $`k=1`$ the upper bound is zero, so $`I(X_1;X_3)=0`$. Directly, $`p(x_3\mid x_1)=p(x_3\mid X_2=1)`$ is independent of $`x_1`$, proving independence of the endpoints.

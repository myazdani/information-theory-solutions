# Exercise 2.15: Information in a Markov chain

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 46. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

The mutual-information chain rule gives
```math
I(X_1;X_2,\ldots,X_n)
=I(X_1;X_2)+I(X_1;X_3,\ldots,X_n\mid X_2).
```
Conditioning on $`X_2`$ separates the past $`X_1`$ from the entire future under the stated factorization. The second term is zero. The answer is therefore $`I(X_1;X_2)`$.

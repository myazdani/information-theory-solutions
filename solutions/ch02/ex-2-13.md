# Exercise 2.13: A logarithmic inequality

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 46. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Set $`f(x)=\ln x-1+1/x`$. Then $`f'(x)=(x-1)/x^2`$. It is decreasing up to 1 and increasing after 1, and $`f(1)=0`$. Thus $`\ln x\ge1-1/x`$ for every positive $`x`$, with equality exactly at 1.

# Exercise 2.36: Different laws with equal directed divergences

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 51. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Choose $`p=(a,1-a)`$ and $`q=(1-a,a)`$, where $`0<a<1`$ and $`a\ne1/2`$. Swapping the two alphabet labels exchanges the laws, while leaving relative entropy invariant. Explicitly,
```math
D(p\Vert q)=D(q\Vert p)=(2a-1)\log_2\frac a{1-a}.
```
For example $`a=1/4`$ gives two distinct laws and a common finite divergence $`\tfrac12\log_2 3`$.

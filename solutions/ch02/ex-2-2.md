# Exercise 2.2: Entropy under transformations

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 44. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
The transformation $`x\mapsto 2^x`$ is injective on the real-valued support. It merely relabels the masses, so $`H(Y)=H(X)`$.

## (b)
The cosine map is deterministic, so $`H(Y)\le H(X)`$. More precisely,
```math
H(X)-H(Y)=H(X\mid Y)\ge0.
```
Equality holds exactly when cosine is injective on the positive-probability support; otherwise some positive masses are merged and the inequality is strict.

# Exercise 2.33: Fano without observations

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 51. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

For fixed $`P_e`$, the leading mass is $`p_1=1-P_e`$ and the other $`m-1`$ masses sum to $`P_e`$. Concavity, or Gibbs' inequality against their uniform conditional distribution, shows
```math
H(p)=h_2(P_e)+P_eH\left(\frac{p_2}{P_e},\ldots,\frac{p_m}{P_e}\right)
\le h_2(P_e)+P_e\log_2(m-1).
```
Equality holds when $`p_2=\cdots=p_m=P_e/(m-1)`$. This law respects the stipulated ordering precisely when $`0\le P_e\le1-1/m`$, the feasible range for the minimum-error predictor.

For $`m\ge2`$, define $`F_m(e)=h_2(e)+e\log_2(m-1)`$. It increases on $`[0,1-1/m]`$ because $`F_m'(e)=\log_2((1-e)(m-1)/e)\ge0`$. Therefore $`P_e\ge F_m^{-1}(H(p))`$. The simpler but weaker bound $`P_e\ge(H(p)-1)/\log_2(m-1)`$ follows from $`h_2(e)\le1`$ when $`m>2`$; for $`m=2`$ use the inverse binary-entropy bound. If $`m=1`$, both entropy and error are zero.

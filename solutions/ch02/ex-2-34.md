# Exercise 2.34: Losing information about the initial state

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 51. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

For each $`n`$, the Markov property gives $`X_0\to X_n\to X_{n+1}`$. Conditional on $`X_n`$, observing $`X_{n+1}`$ does not change the law of $`X_0`$. Thus
```math
H(X_0\mid X_{n+1})\ge H(X_0\mid X_n,X_{n+1})=H(X_0\mid X_n).
```
This direct conditional-entropy proof works also for countable alphabets with infinite entropies. When $`H(X_0)<\infty`$, it is equivalently the data-processing inequality $`I(X_0;X_{n+1})\le I(X_0;X_n)`$.

# Exercise 2.24: Average entropy on a simplex

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 48–49. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
Direct expansion gives
```math
h_2(1/4)=2-\frac34\log_2 3\approx0.8113\ \text{bits}.
```
Using the supplied rounded value 1.584 gives 0.812 bits. Equivalently, partition four equiprobable outcomes into one exceptional outcome and three ordinary ones: $`2=h_2(1/4)+(3/4)\log_2 3`$.

## (b)
Symmetry and integration by parts yield
```math
\int_0^1h_2(p)\,dp
=-\frac2{\ln2}\int_0^1p\ln p\,dp
=\frac1{2\ln2}\approx0.721348.
```

## (c)
Uniform on the $`n`$-simplex means Dirichlet $`(1,\ldots,1)`$. For $`n\ge2`$, one coordinate has density $`(n-1)(1-x)^{n-2}`$. Consequently
```math
\mathbb EH(p_1,\ldots,p_n)
=-\frac{n(n-1)}{\ln2}\int_0^1x\ln x(1-x)^{n-2}\,dx
=\frac{\mathcal H_n-1}{\ln2},
```
where $`\mathcal H_n=\sum_{j=1}^n1/j`$. To evaluate the integral, differentiate
$`\int_0^1x^{a-1}(1-x)^{n-2}dx=(n-2)!/[a(a+1)\cdots(a+n-2)]`$ at $`a=2`$; its value there is $`1/[n(n-1)]`$ and its logarithmic derivative is $`-\sum_{j=2}^n1/j`$. The requested three-dimensional average is $`5/(6\ln2)\approx1.202246`$ bits. For $`n=1`$, both entropy and the formula are zero.

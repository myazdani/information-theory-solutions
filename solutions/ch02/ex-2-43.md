# Exercise 2.43: Information between physical faces

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 53. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
The bottom face is determined by the top face and vice versa. Each is a fair binary variable, so their mutual information is 1 bit.

## (b)
For a fair die with uniformly random orientation among its 24 face orientations, each top face is uniform over six choices. Given the top face, the front face is uniform over the four adjacent faces; it cannot be the top or the opposite face. Thus
```math
I(\text{top};\text{front})=\log_2 6-\log_2 4=\log_2(3/2)\approx0.584963\ \text{bits}.
```
This uses the usual symmetric-orientation interpretation of a fair roll. Merely specifying a uniform top face without a distribution of orientations would not by itself determine the front-face law.

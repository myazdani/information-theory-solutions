# Exercise 2.39: Pairwise independence needs only two joint bits

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 52. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
By pairwise independence and fairness, $`H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y)=2`$. Adding another coordinate cannot reduce entropy, so $`H(X,Y,Z)\ge2`$.

## (b)
Take independent fair bits $`X,Y`$ and set $`Z=X\oplus Y`$. All three pairs are uniform on their four possible values, hence independent. Since $`Z`$ is determined by the first two bits, $`H(X,Y,Z)=2`$, attaining the lower bound.

# Exercise 2.45: A logarithmic moment controls entropy

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 53. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Use the probability distribution $`q_n=1/[n(n+1)]`$, $`n\ge1`$; its sum telescopes to 1. Its cross-entropy under the law of $`X`$ satisfies
```math
\mathbb E[-\log_2q_X]=\mathbb E\log_2[X(X+1)]
\le1+2\mathbb E\log_2X\lt \infty.
```
For complete rigor, partition the positive integers into $`\{1\},\ldots,\{N\},\{N+1,N+2,\ldots\}`$. Gibbs' inequality bounds the entropy of this finite partition by its cross-entropy under the corresponding $`q`$-partition. That cross-entropy is no larger than the full cross-entropy, because the $`q`$ mass of the tail is at least each individual tail mass. As $`N\to\infty`$, the partition entropies increase to $`H(X)`$. Hence
```math
H(X)\le1+2\mathbb E\log_2X\lt \infty.
```
No prior assumption that $`H(X)`$ is finite is used.

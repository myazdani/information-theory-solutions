# Exercise 2.40: Entropy under an invertible pair transformation

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 52. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
Uniformity on eight points gives $`H(X)=\log_2 8=3`$ bits.

## (b)
For the specified geometric law,
```math
H(Y)=\sum_{k\ge1}k2^{-k}=2\ \text{bits}.
```

## (c)
The map $`(x,y)\mapsto(u,v)=(x+y,x-y)`$ is one-to-one: its inverse is $`x=(u+v)/2`$, $`y=(u-v)/2`$ on the attainable support. Relabeling preserves joint entropy. Independence then gives
```math
H(X+Y,X-Y)=H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y)=5\ \text{bits}.
```

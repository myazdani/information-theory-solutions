# Exercise 2.19: A distribution of infinite entropy

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 48. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Use the book's base-2 logarithms and let $`p_n=1/(An(\log_2n)^2)`$. The normalizing constant is positive and finite by the integral test. Since
```math
-\log_2p_n=\log_2A+\log_2n+2\log_2\log_2n,
```
there is an $`n_0`$ such that this expression is at least $`\tfrac12\log_2n`$ for $`n\ge n_0`$. Consequently
```math
H(X)\ge\frac1{2A}\sum_{n\ge n_0}\frac1{n\log_2n}=+\infty.
```
The last divergence follows from the integral test. This lower-bound proof avoids subtracting divergent series.

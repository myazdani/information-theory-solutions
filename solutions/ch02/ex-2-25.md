# Exercise 2.25: Signed three-variable information

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 49. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Take independent fair bits $`X,Y`$ and set $`Z=X\oplus Y`$. Then $`I(X;Y)=0`$ and $`I(X;Y\mid Z)=1`$, so the proposed three-variable quantity is $`-1`$ bit.

## (a)
Expanding the defining difference directly gives
```math
I(X;Y;Z)=H(X)+H(Y)+H(Z)-H(X,Y)-H(Y,Z)-H(Z,X)+H(X,Y,Z).
```
Meanwhile the sum of the three pairwise mutual informations is
```math
I(X;Y)+I(Y;Z)+I(Z;X)
=2[H(X)+H(Y)+H(Z)]-[H(X,Y)+H(Y,Z)+H(Z,X)].
```
Substitute this expression into $`H(X,Y,Z)-H(X)-H(Y)-H(Z)`$ plus that sum; the result is the defining difference above, proving (a).

## (b)
The first displayed expansion is exactly (b). Its invariance under permutations also proves symmetry. These algebraic forms presume finite entropies; they cannot be used to subtract infinities.

# Exercise 2.38: Information in a membership answer

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 52. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

The answer $`Y`$ is a deterministic function of $`X`$ and is Bernoulli with parameter $`\alpha`$. Therefore
```math
H(X)-H(X\mid Y)=I(X;Y)=H(Y)-H(Y\mid X)=h_2(\alpha).
```
For finite $`H(X)`$ this is the requested entropy decrease. It depends only on the total probability of the queried set, not its individual elements. It is largest (1 bit) when the two possible answers are equally likely. If $`H(X)=\infty`$, the difference of entropies should instead be understood via the well-defined mutual information.

# Exercise 2.14: Entropy after adding variables

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 46. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
For each fixed $`X=x`$, the map $`y\mapsto x+y`$ is a bijection on the conditional support. It preserves that conditional probability vector. Averaging gives $`H(Z\mid X)=H(Y\mid X)`$. If $`X,Y`$ are independent, then
```math
H(Z)\ge H(Z\mid X)=H(Y),\qquad H(Z)\ge H(X)
```
by the symmetric argument.

## (b)
Let $`X`$ be a fair bit and $`Y=-X`$. Both have entropy 1, but $`Z=0`$ has entropy 0.

## (c)
The identity
```math
H(X)+H(Y)-H(Z)=I(X;Y)+H(X,Y\mid Z)
```
shows that equality is equivalent to both independence and unique recovery of the supported pair $`(X,Y)`$ from its sum. For independent variables this means that distinct pairs in the Cartesian product of their supports have distinct sums. For example independent uniform $`X\in\{0,1\}`$ and $`Y\in\{0,2\}`$ satisfy both requirements.

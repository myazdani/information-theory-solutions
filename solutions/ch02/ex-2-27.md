# Exercise 2.27: Merging two masses

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 49–50. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Put $`s=p_{m-1}+p_m`$. All other terms cancel between $`H(p)`$ and $`H(q)`$. When $`s>0`$,
```math
H(p)-H(q)=-p_{m-1}\log_2p_{m-1}-p_m\log_2p_m+s\log_2s
=s\left[-\frac{p_{m-1}}s\log_2\frac{p_{m-1}}s-\frac{p_m}s\log_2\frac{p_m}s\right].
```
This is the stated grouping identity. If $`s=0`$, the difference and the weighted conditional-entropy term are both zero by continuity.

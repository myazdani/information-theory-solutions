# Exercise 8.1 — Three differential entropies

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 256–257.

## Solution

The requested entropies use natural logarithms and are in nats.

### (a)
For an exponential variable, $`-\ln f(X)=-\ln\lambda+\lambda X`$ and $`\mathbb EX=1/\lambda`$. Thus $`h(X)=1-\ln\lambda`$.

### (b)
For the two-sided exponential density, $`-\ln f(X)=\ln(2/\lambda)+\lambda|X|`$. Since $`\mathbb E|X|=1/\lambda`$,
```math
h(X)=1+\ln(2/\lambda).
```
### (c)
The independent Gaussian sum has mean $`\mu_1+\mu_2`$ and variance $`\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2`$. Its entropy is
```math
h(X_1+X_2)=\tfrac12\ln\bigl(2\pi e(\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2)\bigr).
```
This assumes a positive summed variance; a deterministic sum has no ordinary Lebesgue density.

# Exercise 8.2 — Concavity of log determinant

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 257.

## Solution

First assume $`K_1,K_2`$ positive definite and $`0<\lambda<1`$. Independently choose $`\Theta`$ with probabilities $`\lambda,1-\lambda`$, and let $`Z`$ conditionally have distribution $`N(0,K_\Theta)`$. Its covariance is $`K=\lambda K_1+(1-\lambda)K_2`$. Conditioning reduces entropy, and the Gaussian maximizes entropy at a fixed covariance, so
```math
\frac\lambda2\ln((2\pi e)^n\det K_1)
+\frac{1-\lambda}2\ln((2\pi e)^n\det K_2)
=h(Z\mid\Theta)\le h(Z)\le\frac12\ln((2\pi e)^n\det K).
```
Cancel the common term and exponentiate to obtain
```math
\det(\lambda K_1+(1-\lambda)K_2)
\ge(\det K_1)^\lambda(\det K_2)^{1-\lambda}.
```
For positive semidefinite matrices apply the proved result to $`K_i+\delta I`$ and let $`\delta\downarrow0`$, using continuity of the determinant. At $`\lambda=0,1`$ the result is equality, interpreted directly instead of assigning an ambiguous $`0^0`$.

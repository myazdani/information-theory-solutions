# Exercise 8.8 — Five inputs in uniform additive noise

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 258.

## Solution

The output lies in $`[-3,3]`$, so $`h_2(Y)\le\log_2 6`$. Since the noise has entropy $`h_2(Z)=1`$, every input distribution satisfies
```math
I(X;Y)=h_2(Y)-1\le\log_2 3.
```
Assign equal mass $`1/3`$ to the three inputs $`-2,0,2`$ and zero to $`-1,1`$. The conditional output intervals are $`[-3,-1]`$, $`[-1,1]`$, and $`[1,3]`$, with disjoint interiors, and their mixture is uniform on $`[-3,3]`$. Therefore
```math
C=\log_2 3\text{ bits per use}.
```
The endpoints of the intervals have probability zero, so overlaps there do not prevent exact decoding of the chosen three-symbol alphabet.

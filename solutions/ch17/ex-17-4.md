# Exercise 17.4: A four-variable data-processing refinement

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 687.

## Solution

Assume the displayed mutual informations are finite; equivalently one can use conditional-information identities to avoid subtracting infinities. The Markov property gives

```math
I(X_2;X_3)-I(X_1;X_3)=I(X_2;X_3\mid X_1),
\qquad
I(X_2;X_4)-I(X_1;X_4)=I(X_2;X_4\mid X_1).
```

Conditionally on $`X_1`$, the chain $`X_2\to X_3\to X_4`$ still holds, so conditional data processing implies

```math
I(X_2;X_3\mid X_1)\ge I(X_2;X_4\mid X_1).
```

Rearrangement proves the inequality. More specifically, the right side of the requested inequality minus its left side is exactly $`I(X_2;X_3\mid X_1,X_4)\ge0`$.

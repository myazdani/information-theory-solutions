# Exercise 17.1: Determinant monotonicity

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 686.

## Solution

Put $`A=K_1^{-1/2}K_2K_1^{-1/2}`$. This matrix is positive definite, so its eigenvalues $`\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n`$ are strictly positive. Factoring the determinant gives

```math
\det(K_1+K_2)=\det K_1\det(I+A)
=\det K_1\prod_{i=1}^n(1+\lambda_i)>\det K_1.
```

This proves the requested weak inequality and in fact gives strict inequality when both matrices are positive definite. For merely positive semidefinite $`K_2`$, the weak inequality remains true, with equality exactly when $`K_2=0`$ (assuming $`K_1`$ remains positive definite).

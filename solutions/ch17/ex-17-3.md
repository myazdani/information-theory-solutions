# Exercise 17.3: Convexity of a log determinant difference

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 687.

## Solution

Let $`f(K)=\ln\det(K+K_0)-\ln\det K`$, with fixed $`K_0\succ0`$. Along any symmetric direction $`H`$ that stays in the positive definite cone,

```math
D^2f(K)[H,H]=\mathrm{tr}(K^{-1}HK^{-1}H)
-\mathrm{tr}((K+K_0)^{-1}H(K+K_0)^{-1}H).
```

Set $`A=K^{-1}`$ and $`B=(K+K_0)^{-1}`$. Order reversal under inversion gives $`A\succeq B\succ0`$. Moreover

```math
A\otimes A-B\otimes B=(A-B)\otimes A+B\otimes(A-B)\succeq0.
```

Each Kronecker product on the right is positive semidefinite. Vectorization of the quadratic forms above consequently gives

```math
D^2f(K)[H,H]=\mathrm{vec}(H)^T
(A\otimes A-B\otimes B)\mathrm{vec}(H)\ge0.
```

Since the positive definite cone is convex, nonnegativity of every directional second derivative proves convexity. The same proof applies to Hermitian matrices using conjugate transpose and the corresponding conjugated Kronecker factor.

# Exercise 12.3: White noise and a Gaussian autoregression

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 421.

## Solution

Entropies are in bits. Spectra use angular frequency $`\omega\in[-\pi,\pi]`$ and normalization $`R_k=(2\pi)^{-1}\int S(\omega)e^{ik\omega}d\omega`$.

### (a)
The maximum rate is $`\tfrac12\log_2(2\pi e)`$, achieved by independent $`N(0,1)`$ variables. Indeed the chain rule and the Gaussian entropy bound give

```math
h(X_1^n)\le\sum_{i=1}^n h(X_i)
\le\frac n2\log_2(2\pi e\,EX_i^2)
=\frac n2\log_2(2\pi e).
```

### (b)
For every admissible process, $`E(X_i-X_{i-1}/2)^2=3/4`$. Translation under conditioning, conditioning reducing entropy, and the Gaussian bound imply

```math
h(X_i\mid X_1^{i-1})
\le h(X_i-X_{i-1}/2)
\le\tfrac12\log_2(2\pi e\,3/4).
```

This is attained by the stationary Gaussian recursion
$`X_i=X_{i-1}/2+Z_i`$, with independent innovations $`Z_i\sim N(0,3/4)`$ and stationary variance 1. Its lag-one second moment is $`1/2`$.

### (c)
The maximizing spectra are respectively

```math
S_a(\omega)=1,\qquad
S_b(\omega)=\frac{3/4}{|1-\tfrac12e^{-i\omega}|^2}
=\frac{3/4}{5/4-\cos\omega}.
```

These expressions also verify the required lag-zero and lag-one moments by the covariance sequence $`R_k=2^{-|k|}`$ in part (b).

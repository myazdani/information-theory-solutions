# Exercise 12.17: A spin chain with prescribed correlation

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 424.

## Solution

### (a)
Since $`X_iX_{i+1}`$ is +1 on agreement and -1 on disagreement,
$`EX_iX_{i+1}=1/2`$ is equivalent to agreement probability $`3/4`$. The maximum-rate process is the stationary symmetric Markov chain

```math
P=\begin{pmatrix}3/4&1/4\\1/4&3/4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\pi=(-1,+1)\text{ with probabilities }(1/2,1/2).
```

For any admissible process, put $`B_i=1\{X_i\ne X_{i-1}\}`$. Then
$`H(X_i\mid X_1^{i-1})\le H(B_i)=h_2(1/4)`$. Independent flip indicators attain equality and produce the displayed chain.

### (b)
The maximum entropy rate is $`h_2(1/4)=2-\tfrac34\log_2 3`$ bits per symbol.

### (c)
Yes. For independent identically distributed spins with $`P(X_i=+1)=p`$,
$`EX_iX_{i+1}=(2p-1)^2`$. Thus either

```math
p=\frac{1+1/\sqrt2}{2}\quad\text{or}\quad
p=\frac{1-1/\sqrt2}{2}
```

meets the constraint. These Bernoulli processes are feasible but have smaller entropy rate than the maximizing Markov chain.

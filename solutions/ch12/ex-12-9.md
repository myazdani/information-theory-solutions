# Exercise 12.9: A binary chain with prescribed agreement probability

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 423.

## Solution

### (a)
Let $`B_i=1\{X_i=X_{i-1}\}`$. Given $`X_{i-1}`$, specifying $`B_i`$ specifies $`X_i`$. Therefore

```math
H(X_i\mid X_1^{i-1})\le H(B_i)=h_2(1/3).
```

The bound is achieved by a stationary two-state Markov chain with uniform stationary distribution and transition matrix

```math
P=\begin{pmatrix}1/3&2/3\\2/3&1/3\end{pmatrix}.
```

Equivalently, start with a fair bit and use independent agreement indicators, each Bernoulli $`1/3`$. This construction meets the constraint at every time.

### (b)
Its entropy rate is
$`h_2(1/3)=\log_2 3-2/3`$ bits per symbol. The single initial bit contributes $`1/n`$ to the block entropy rate and disappears asymptotically.

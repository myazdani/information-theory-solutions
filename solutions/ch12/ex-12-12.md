# Exercise 12.12: An entropy lower bound on prediction risk

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 423.

## Solution

### (a)
Let $`V=X^n`$, $`E=X_{n+1}-E[X_{n+1}\mid V]`$, and $`\sigma^2=EE^2`$. Conditional translation leaves entropy unchanged. With base-two entropy,

```math
h(X_{n+1}\mid V)=h(E\mid V)\le h(E)
\le\tfrac12\log_2(2\pi e\sigma^2).
```

Thus

```math
E\bigl[E((X_{n+1}-\widehat X_{n+1})^2\mid X^n)\bigr]
\ge\frac{2^{2h(X_{n+1}\mid X^n)}}{2\pi e}.
```

The assertion is interpreted for finite conditional entropy and finite risk, with the usual limiting convention for entropy $`-\infty`$.

### (b)
Yes, for a jointly Gaussian process with nonsingular prediction error. The conditional mean is affine in the past, and the residual is Gaussian and independent of the past. Both entropy inequalities are then equalities. Stationarity is unnecessary. More generally, equality requires a Gaussian residual with constant conditional variance independent of the past; joint Gaussianity is sufficient but not necessary.

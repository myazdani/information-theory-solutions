# Exercise 12.4: Independence under fixed marginals

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 421.

## Solution

For fixed marginals $`r`$ and $`c`$,
$`H(X,Y)=H(r)+H(c)-I(X;Y)\le H(r)+H(c)`$.
Equality holds exactly when $`p_{ij}=r_i c_j`$. Reading the row and column probabilities in the printed figure gives
$`r=(1/2,1/4,1/4)`$ and $`c=(2/3,1/6,1/6)`$. Therefore

```math
p_* =\begin{pmatrix}
1/3&1/12&1/12\\
1/6&1/24&1/24\\
1/6&1/24&1/24
\end{pmatrix}.
```

Its row and column sums are the specified marginals, and its entropy is
$`\tfrac32+H(2/3,1/6,1/6)`$ bits. The argument proves the general result for any finite fixed marginals.

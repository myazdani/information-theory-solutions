# Exercise 12.16: Entropy rate from two covariance constraints

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 424.

## Solution

For each $`i\ge2`$, set $`E_i=X_i-X_{i-1}/2`$. The constraints give

```math
EE_i^2=1+\tfrac14-EX_iX_{i-1}=\tfrac34.
```

Therefore

```math
h(X_i\mid X_1^{i-1})=h(E_i\mid X_1^{i-1})
\le h(E_i)\le\tfrac12\log_2(2\pi e\,3/4).
```

Also $`h(X_1)\le\tfrac12\log_2(2\pi e)`$. Summing and dividing by $`n`$ proves the entropy-rate upper bound
$`\tfrac12\log_2(3\pi e/2)`$ bits per sample. It is attained by the stationary Gaussian AR(1) process
$`X_i=X_{i-1}/2+Z_i`$, where the innovations are independent $`N(0,3/4)`$ and independent of the past. The stationary variance is $`(3/4)/(1-1/4)=1`$, and its lag-one covariance is $`1/2`$.

# Exercise 12.19: Binary maximum entropy under fixed agreement

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 424.

## Solution

### (a)
A maximizing process has a fair initial state and independent transition decisions: stay with probability $`1/3`$, flip with probability $`2/3`$. The stationary transition matrix is

```math
P=\begin{pmatrix}1/3&2/3\\2/3&1/3\end{pmatrix}.
```

For any other feasible process, the indicator $`A_i=1\{X_i=X_{i-1}\}`$ has entropy $`h_2(1/3)`$. Given the preceding bit, this indicator determines the next bit. Hence

```math
H(X_1^n)\le H(X_1)+\sum_{i=2}^nH(A_i)
\le1+(n-1)h_2(1/3).
```

Our independent-decision chain attains the bound exactly.

### (b)
Dividing by $`n`$ gives entropy rate $`h_2(1/3)=\log_2 3-2/3`$ bits. This exercise repeats the constraint in Exercise 12.9; the proof here is included independently.

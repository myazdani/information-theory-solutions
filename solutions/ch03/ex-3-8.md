# Exercise 3.8: The geometric mean of a discrete sample

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 66. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

All sample values are positive and have integrable logarithms. By the strong law,
```math
\frac1n\log_2\prod_{i=1}^nX_i\longrightarrow
\frac12\log_21+\frac14\log_22+\frac14\log_23
=\frac14\log_26.
```
Exponentiating gives almost-sure limit $`6^{1/4}\approx1.5650845801`$.

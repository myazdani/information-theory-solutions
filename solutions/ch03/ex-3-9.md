# Exercise 3.9: A mismatched model on iid data

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 67. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

## (a)
If $`q(x)>0`$ whenever $`p(x)>0`$, the finite-alphabet strong law gives
```math
-\frac1n\log_2q(X^n)\longrightarrow-\sum_xp(x)\log_2q(x)=H(p)+D(p\Vert q)
```
almost surely. If some positive-$`p`$ symbol has zero $`q`$ mass, that symbol appears eventually almost surely; the expression is then infinite from that time onward. The same extended-real formula applies.

## (b)
Under the first support condition,
```math
\frac1n\log_2\frac{q(X^n)}{p(X^n)}
\longrightarrow\sum_xp(x)\log_2\frac{q(x)}{p(x)}=-D(p\Vert q).
```
Under support mismatch, the ratio becomes zero eventually almost surely and its normalized log is $`-\infty`$. For distinct compatible laws the limiting exponent is strictly negative; for identical laws it is zero.

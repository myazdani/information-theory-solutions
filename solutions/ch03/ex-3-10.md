# Exercise 3.10: A random box and its equivalent cube

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 67. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

For independent uniform $`X_i\in[0,1]`$,
```math
\mathbb E\ln X_i=\int_0^1\ln x\,dx=-1,
\qquad\mathbb E|\ln X_i|=1.
```
The strong law thus gives $`V_n^{1/n}\to e^{-1}\approx0.3678794412`$ almost surely. In contrast, independence gives $`\mathbb EV_n=(\mathbb EX_1)^n=2^{-n}`$, so $`(\mathbb EV_n)^{1/n}=1/2`$ for every $`n`$. Averages of volumes are affected by atypically large products and do not give the typical equivalent edge length. The two expressions must also be distinguished from $`\mathbb E[V_n^{1/n}]`$, which converges to $`e^{-1}`$ by bounded convergence.

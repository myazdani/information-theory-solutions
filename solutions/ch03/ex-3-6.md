# Exercise 3.6: The geometric probability of an observed block

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 66. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

The AEP, or the strong law for $`-\log_2p(X_i)`$, yields
```math
p(X_1,\ldots,X_n)^{1/n}
=2^{-\frac1n\sum_i[-\log_2p(X_i)]}\longrightarrow2^{-H(X)}
```
almost surely when $`H(X)<\infty`$. If $`H(X)=\infty`$, apply the strong law to bounded truncations of the nonnegative surprisal; its sample average tends to infinity almost surely. The limit is then zero, agreeing with $`2^{-\infty}=0`$.

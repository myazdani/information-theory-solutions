# Exercise 3.3: The surviving cake fraction

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed pp. 65. Logarithms are base 2 unless explicitly written as ln.

## Solution

Let $`F_i`$ be the fraction retained at cut $`i`$. The largest-piece rule gives $`F_i=2/3`$ with probability $`3/4`$ and $`F_i=3/5`$ with probability $`1/4`$. For independent cuts the remaining fraction is $`V_n=\prod_iF_i`$. The strong law gives
```math
\frac1n\log_2V_n\longrightarrow\frac34\log_2\frac23+\frac14\log_2\frac35=-c,
\qquad c\approx0.6229632741.
```
Hence, to first order in the exponent,
```math
V_n=2^{-n(c+o(1))}=\left[(2/3)^{3/4}(3/5)^{1/4}\right]^{n+o(n)},
```
with geometric factor approximately $`0.6493358310`$. This describes a typical sample path; an expectation of the retained fraction would be a different quantity.

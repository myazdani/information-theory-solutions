# Exercise 13.6: Compression of a constant string

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 458.

## Solution

### (a)
The complete LZ78 phrases are
$`1,11,111,1111,\ldots,1^k`$, followed if necessary by a shorter final reference. The largest complete phrase index satisfies $`k(k+1)/2\le n`$, so $`k=\lfloor(\sqrt{8n+1}-1)/2\rfloor`$. The pairs for complete phrases are $`(0,1),(1,1),\ldots,(k-1,1)`$.

### (b)
Each of the $`k`$ records needs at most $`\lceil\log_2(k+1)\rceil+1`$ bits for its pointer and literal; framing and the final reference cost $`O(\log n)`$ bits. Thus

```math
\frac{L_n}{n}\le
\frac{k(\lceil\log_2(k+1)\rceil+1)+O(\log n)}n
=O\left(\frac{\log n}{\sqrt n}\right)\longrightarrow0.
```

This matches the zero entropy rate of a deterministic constant source.

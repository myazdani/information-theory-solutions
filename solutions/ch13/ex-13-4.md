# Exercise 13.4: The arithmetic interval of a five-symbol prefix

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 458.

## Solution

The stationary marginal is uniform. Using the specified Markov transitions, successive refinement gives

```math
I(0)=[0,1/2),\quad
I(01)=[1/6,1/2),\quad
I(011)=[7/18,1/2),\quad
I(0111)=[25/54,1/2),\quad
I(01110)=[25/54,79/162).
```

### (a)
The cumulative probability strictly before the requested source prefix is its interval's left endpoint, so $`F(01110)=25/54`$.

### (b)
Every possible infinite continuation maps into the final interval. This interval lies inside $`[7/16,1/2)`$, whose binary prefix is 0111. It crosses $`15/32`$, the boundary between the five-bit cells 01110 and 01111. Consequently exactly **four** encoded bits, **0111**, are determined without knowing the continuation. All allowed finite source continuations have positive probability, so both sides of that five-bit boundary are genuinely possible.

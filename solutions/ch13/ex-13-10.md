# Exercise 13.10: Two asymptotically optimal fixed databases

Source: Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd ed. (2006), printed p. 459.

## Solution

Write all logarithms in base two, and let $`H`$ be the iid source entropy. Zero-probability strings are irrelevant.

### (a)
For the usual recurrence index, which measures a backward shift of the length-$`l`$ block and permits overlap, Kac's recurrence formula gives

```math
E[R_l\mid X^l=x^l]=1/P(x^l).
```

A $`\delta`$-typical string has $`P(x^l)\ge2^{-l(H+\delta)}`$, proving the claimed bound. If one defines recurrence to require a wholly disjoint past occurrence, that exact Kac identity no longer applies; the simple iid bound becomes $`l/P(x^l)`$. The distinction does not change either asymptotic result below.

### (b)
Choose $`0<\delta<\epsilon`$ and split by typicality. Markov's inequality gives

```math
P\{R_l>2^{l(H+\epsilon)}\}
\le P\{X^l\notin T_\delta^{(l)}\}
 +2^{-l(\epsilon-\delta)}\longrightarrow0.
```

The first term vanishes by the AEP. If only disjoint matches are used, the second term is multiplied by $`l`$ and still vanishes.

### (c.i) A dictionary of typical strings
There are at most $`2^{l(H+\delta)}`$ typical blocks. A block index therefore costs at most $`l(H+\delta)+1`$ bits, plus one flag. Concatenating these blocks into a literal database instead adds at most $`\log_2l+O(1)`$ pointer bits. Nontypical blocks are sent literally using $`\lceil l\log_2A\rceil`$ bits. Hence the expected rate is at most

```math
H+\delta+O(\log l/l)
 +P\{X^l\notin T_\delta^{(l)}\}\log_2A.
```

### (c.ii) A recent random database
Take database length $`L=\lceil2^{l(H+\delta)}\rceil`$. Its pointer cost is $`l(H+\delta)+O(1)`$. To ensure matches lie entirely inside the past, use $`\lfloor L/l\rfloor`$ disjoint length-$`l`$ database blocks. Conditional on a $`\delta/2`$-typical target, each independent block matches with probability at least $`2^{-l(H+\delta/2)}`$, so

```math
P\{\text{no match}\mid\text{typical target}\}
\le\exp\{-\lfloor L/l\rfloor2^{-l(H+\delta/2)}\}\longrightarrow0.
```

The total escape probability vanishes by the AEP. Sending unmatched blocks literally thus yields expected rate at most $`H+\delta+o(1)`$.

For either database, the target is iid and independent of the known database; the expected length of a uniquely decodable description is at least $`lH`$. First let $`l\to\infty`$ and then $`\delta\downarrow0`$ (or select a diagonal sequence) to obtain rate $`H`$. A fixed positive $`\delta`$ alone proves a bound within $`\delta`$, not exact convergence to $`H`$; this order-of-limits qualification is necessary.
